# How we treat each other

Thornswood has one set of rules for being decent to each other, and they hold
here on GitHub the same as in the Discord and in the world. They are the
"In the Discord" list on [thornswood.net/rules](https://thornswood.net/rules).
In short:

- Be decent to each other. No harassment, no slurs, no personal attacks and no
  hate of any kind, aimed at what a person is. That covers issues, pull
  requests, comments, names and pictures. Arguing about the work is fine.
  Arguing about each other is not.
- Keep it fit for everyone here. Nothing sexual and nothing graphic.
- Do not post anybody's private details: their real name, address, email,
  accounts or anything they told you in private.
- Thornswood is eighteen and over. The rules page says what that means.
- If staff ask you to stop, stop first and argue after.

## On GitHub

Staff may edit or hide a comment that breaks these, lock a conversation, close
an issue or a pull request, or block an account from the repository.

## Telling us

Report somebody on [thornswood.net/report](https://thornswood.net/report),
signed in, or send a direct message to a staff member in the
[Discord](https://thornswood.net/discord). Never as an issue: everybody can
read those.
