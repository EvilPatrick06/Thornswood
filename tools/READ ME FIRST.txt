SKYRIM TOGETHER
===============

Run SkyrimTogether-Setup.exe, with SkyrimTogether-Setup.zip sitting in the same
folder. Do not unzip it yourself, the exe does that.

Windows will probably say "Windows protected your PC" because the file is not
signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". That warning appears for any
program without a code-signing certificate.


What it does
------------
Builds a complete, self-contained Skyrim on the drive you pick, with our whole
mod list already set up. You do not install mods. You do not run Nemesis. You
do not need a Nexus account, Vortex, or Mod Organizer.

Your Steam copy of Skyrim is only READ. Nothing there is renamed, patched or
deleted. If you play another server or anything else, it keeps working exactly
as it does now.


What you need
-------------
- Skyrim Special Edition installed on Steam, on this PC
- About 32 GB free on whichever drive you choose
- Steam signed in and running


One thing it may ask you to do
------------------------------
Our server runs game build 1.6.1170 and Steam ships a newer one. If your copy
is newer, the installer stops and shows three download_depot commands, already
on your clipboard. Paste them into the Steam console ONE AT A TIME and wait for
each to print Done. There is no progress bar. Then press Enter in the installer
window.

That is Steam handing you an older build of a game you already own. It
downloads into Steam's own folder and does not change your installed game.


Playing
-------
You get a "Thornswood" shortcut on your desktop. Open it and press PLAY.

First time only:
- Make a new character and NAME IT AFTER YOURSELF. That name is what the rest
  of us see over your head in game.
- Save once before you connect.

Then connect to the address shown in the launcher. Ask in the Discord for the password.


While you play
--------------
The launcher points Skyrim's settings and saves folders at this build while the
game is running, and puts them back the second you quit. If the game crashes
hard, just open the launcher again and it cleans that up by itself. The "Unlink"
button does it on demand.


If something goes wrong
-----------------------
The launcher shows five checks. Any red row is the problem. "Repair" puts the
settings files back to the ones that shipped. Screenshot the launcher and send
it to the Discord.

Do not run Steam's "Verify Integrity of Game Files" on Skyrim. It drags your
Steam copy to the newest build. It cannot hurt our folder, but it is a bad
habit while any of this is set up.
