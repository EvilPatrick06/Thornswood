# Supporting Thornswood

Thornswood is free and always will be. Nothing here makes your character
stronger, your swing harder or your loot better, and nothing behind a tier
changes anything another person has to live with.

What it costs to run is a server, a domain and the time.

## The tiers

| | Milk Drinker | Talos Worshiper | Thalmor Boot-Licker |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A month** | $3 | $7 | $15 |
| **Characters** | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| **Name colour in the Discord** | yes | yes | yes |
| **Tickets answered first** | | yes | yes |
| **Release notes before they go out** | | yes | yes |
| **Your name in the credits** | | | yes |
| **A vote on what gets built next** | | | yes |

Everybody gets **two characters** without paying anything.

The names get worse the more you pay. That is the joke and we are keeping it.

Your tier role appears in the Discord on its own within a few minutes of
subscribing, and goes away on its own when you stop. Nobody has to hand it out
and nobody has to take it back.

## What it will never get you

Gold. Gear. Skills. Land you did not earn in the world. A title anyone in the
world has to respect. Being un-bannable. Being right in an argument with a
Gamemaster.

If a thing would make somebody else's evening worse, it is not for sale. That
is the whole rule and there is no version of this where it changes.

## Why the characters

It is the one thing that costs nobody anything. An extra character slot takes a
row in a database. It does not take gold out of the economy, it does not put
you ahead of anybody, and the person standing next to you cannot tell.

Everything else on that list is a colour, a queue position, or a thank you.
