Skyrim co-op. One file, one install, and it does not touch the Skyrim you
already have.

DOWNLOAD
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1zESrSYy6s-kzUtCVF6g0RMrfCUvooTaT?usp=drive_link

It is about 11 GB. Google Drive will say it cannot scan a file that size for
viruses. Click "Download anyway".


WHAT YOU NEED
- Skyrim Special Edition on Steam, on this PC
- About 25 GB free
- Steam signed in and running


INSTALL
Run SkyrimTogether-Setup.exe.

Windows will say "Windows protected your PC" because the file is not signed.
More info, then Run anyway. Every unsigned program does this.

Press Install and leave it. It unpacks, copies the base game out of your Steam
folder into C:\SkyrimTogether, and sets that copy up with the whole mod list
already installed. You do not install any mods. You do not need a Nexus
account, Vortex, or Nemesis.

Your Steam copy of Skyrim is only read from. Nothing there is renamed, patched
or deleted, so anything else you play with it keeps working exactly as it does.

One thing it may stop and ask for: our server runs game build 1.6.1170 and
Steam ships a newer one. If yours is newer, the installer shows you three
download_depot commands and puts them on your clipboard. Paste them into the
Steam console one at a time and wait for each to say Done. There is no progress
bar. Then press Enter back in the installer. That is Steam handing you an older
build of a game you already own, into Steam's own folder, and it does not
change your installed game.


PLAYING
You get a "Skyrim Together" shortcut on your desktop.

First time only:
  - Make a new character and NAME IT AFTER YOURSELF. That name is what the rest
    of us see floating over your head, and changing it later means a new
    character.
  - Save once before you connect.

Then press Play. Connect to the address shown in the launcher. Password is in
this chat.

F2 opens the multiplayer menu: party, who is online, teleport to someone, chat.


THINGS TO KNOW
- Sleeping and waiting do nothing. Time is shared across the server.
- Menus do not pause the game. Do not sort your bags mid fight.
- You get hungry, thirsty and cold. Eat, drink, stay warm.
- Party up before starting a quest and let one person lead it.
- Do not run Steam's "Verify Integrity of Game Files" on Skyrim.
- Do not add mods with a plugin. The launcher checks your list before it lets
  you play and will stop you, because a mismatched list desyncs everyone.
  Textures and meshes are fine, there is a folder for them under Mods.

Updates come through the launcher. When the mod list changes you get only the
files that changed, not the whole thing again. Take them when it offers.

If something is wrong, the launcher's Play page shows five checks and any red
row is the actual problem. Screenshot it and send it over.
