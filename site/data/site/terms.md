# Terms

The agreement between you and Thornswood, in the shortest form that is still
honest.

Last changed: 19 October 2026.

Thornswood is a free fan project run by two people in their own time. Using the
site, the launcher, the Discord or the server means you accept what is below.
If you do not, do not use them, and nothing else happens.

## The short version

- Eighteen and over.
- Play by the rules; staff decide, and the decision stands.
- Your account is yours and is not to be shared or sold.
- Nothing here is promised to stay running, and there is no refund because
  there is no charge.
- Nothing here belongs to Bethesda, and Thornswood does not speak for them.

## Who can be here

You must be eighteen or over. There is no way for this project to safely run an
eighteen and over roleplay space with minors in it, so an account found to
belong to somebody under eighteen is removed rather than warned.

Thornswood contains sexual content between adult characters, violence, and language that is not written for children. Confirming on the download or join page, and the checkbox in the installer, are kept as a record of a statement somebody made, not as verification of age.

You also need to own Skyrim Special Edition on Steam. Thornswood ships no game
files. Three files it uses are made on your own computer from your own copy of
the game: the main menu's interface file, `Data\Interface\startmenu.swf`,
which is the game's own with its Bethesda logo and button list taken out, and
the notice board (`noticeboard01.nif`) and drop box (`dropbox01.nif`) meshes,
put together from pieces of Skyrim's own. The launcher makes them from the
game's archives before you play, and none of them is sent to you. Everything
Thornswood ships is its own, or other people's work that the
[credits](/credits) page names, and none of it can be played without the game.

## The rules of the world are part of this

The [rules](/rules) are the substance of this agreement, not an appendix. The
short ones that matter here: nobody's character dies without that person
agreeing, except that staff can end a character for good for serious rule
breaking or in an event its owner agreed to; play a person rather than a
legend, keep what happens in character in character, and staff decide.

Staff can remove access from anybody, at any time, for breaking those or for
making the place worse. You will be told why. There is no appeal body, because
there are two of us. A ban is a decision, not a punishment we enjoy handing
out, and asking about it calmly in the Discord is always allowed.

## Your account

Your account is your Discord account. Do not share it, sell it, rent it, or let
somebody else play your character. A sold or shared account is removed, and so
is the character, because a roleplay server where a name might be anybody is
not a roleplay server.

Do not try to get around a ban with another account.

## What you may not do

- Cheating: modifying the client to do what the server did not allow, exploiting
  a bug for advantage instead of reporting it, or automating play.
- Attacking the service: the server, the site, the voice service or anybody's
  connection to them.
- Harassing anybody, in or out of character. Out of character is never excused
  by in character.
- Anything illegal where you are, or anything that makes Thornswood a party to
  it.

Finding a bug and telling us is the opposite of this and is always welcome. A
security one goes to the owner as a Discord direct message, never in a channel.

## What you make here

Your character, what you write in chat and the pictures you take remain yours.
By putting them in Thornswood you let us keep them on the server, show them to
the people you are playing with, and put a picture in the gallery on the site
if you send it for that. Say so and a picture comes down.

Anything said or written on the server may be read by staff when something is
reported. The [privacy page](/privacy) says what is kept and how to have it
deleted.

## What Thornswood is built on

Thornswood runs on Skyrim Special Edition, SkyMP, Skyrim Platform and a
collection of mods made by other people, and it is built on their work.
[Credits](/credits) names them.

It is a fan project. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with
Bethesda Softworks, ZeniMax or any mod author. It costs nothing to play and
nothing on it is sold.

Bethesda's [terms for mods](https://store.steampowered.com/eula/1946180_eula_0)
say a mod is free and only for people who own the game, and Thornswood is both.
They ask every mod to say this, in their words: THIS MOD IS NOT MADE,
GUARANTEED OR SUPPORTED BY ZENIMAX OR ITS AFFILIATES.

## Supporting it

The Patreon pays for the server, the domain and the other expenses of keeping
Thornswood running, and nothing else. It is not payment for anybody's time
making mods, which Bethesda's terms for mods do not allow. It buys nothing in
the world: no items, no ranks, no advantage, no priority. If that ever changes
it will be announced before it does, not discovered. Patreon handles the
payment and Thornswood never sees a card. Cancelling is done at Patreon.

## No promises about it working

This is said plainly rather than in capital letters: Thornswood is provided as
it is. It is in alpha. The server goes down, characters can be lost,
a wipe may be needed, and the whole thing may stop one day. Nothing here is
guaranteed to be available, correct, or to keep your character safe, and
neither the project nor the people running it owe you anything if it is not.

You install a large collection of mods on your own machine to play. That is
your machine and your decision, and the launcher tells you what it is doing
before it does it.
