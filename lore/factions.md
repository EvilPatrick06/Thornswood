# The Factions of Skyrim, 4E 252

*The factions of Skyrim fifty years after the Crown was settled: who they
are, where they keep, and who leads them.*

Sixteen factions keep their own people in Skyrim today. Some keep halls and
ledgers, and some keep caves. Each has a head, and the head's word is the
faction's word.

The Legion is gone. Article the Third of the Accord says no Legion soldier
sets foot in Skyrim under arms, and none has in thirty years. The Stormcloaks
are gone as well. They became the Crown, the High King's Guard and every
hold's guard. A veteran of either side is a person with a past, not a member
of a faction.

The East Empire Company left in 4E 222, and the Free Skyrim Trading Company
took over its docks. The Penitus Oculatus has no house in Skyrim; what the
Empire learns here, it learns through its one embassy. The Greybeards take
nobody.

---

## Ours: the three the last fifty years made

### The Imperial Embassy

One house in Solitude, with twenty people in service. Six of them are armed,
and only inside the walls (Article the Third). The Ambassador keeps the
Accord, buys grain for the holds and sells it to them, and watches for the
Dominion as closely as any Nord. Imperial law runs inside the embassy's doors
and nowhere else.

*Playing here:* embassy staff, Imperial citizens under its protection,
couriers and clerks. The embassy is lawful and closely watched, and its
people can be sent home for one bad night.

### The Thalmor

Outlawed since the Expulsion of 4E 209. What is left is the Remnant: the
grandchildren of Justiciars who missed the border, running gold and poison
under other names. No order from the Dominion has reached them in forty years
that anybody can prove. The Empire hands them over as readily as a Nord would
(Article the Fifth).

*Playing here:* a hunted people with a long memory. They have no embassy to
run to and no immunity, and their head decides whether the cause is dead or
only sleeping.

### The Free Skyrim Trading Company

The Company rents the old East Empire warehouses from the Jarls, owns no pier
and no ship, and carries goods for anybody who pays. It takes its cut when a
load arrives. Some of its doors still carry the old East Empire signs.

*Playing here:* haulers, buyers, factors and guards for hire. Its head is
given the seat, like a Jarl, and answers to the Jarls whose warehouses it
rents.

## The old halls

### The Companions

Jorrvaskr in Whiterun, as it has been since Ysgramor's day. They are
fighters for hire who put honour before coin. Their Harbinger is chosen by
the Circle, not born to it, and the Circle keeps its own counsel in the
Underforge.

*Playing here:* fighters who sell their swords honestly, and enemies of the
Silver Hand.

### The College of Winterhold

The town around it fell into the sea and the College did not. It is still
the one place in Skyrim where a mage can study without a Jarl's man watching,
and for that reason much of Skyrim still distrusts it. The bridge is the only
way in, and the Arch-Mage decides who crosses.

*Playing here:* apprentices, scholars and masters. Winterhold lives on what
the College spends.

### The Bards College

Solitude's oldest school. It teaches the songs, keeps the histories and
decides, more than any Jarl would like, how a reign is remembered. The
Burning of King Olaf is still held every year.

*Playing here:* singers, historians and the people who write down what
happened.

## The shadows

### The Thieves Guild

The Ratway under Riften. The Guild rebuilt itself from nothing once and has
spent fifty years making sure it never has to again: it keeps a fence in
every hold and favours owed by more than one Jarl. Its Guild Master sits in
the Ragged Flagon.

*Playing here:* thieves, fences and fixers. The Guild's oldest rule is no
killing.

### The Dark Brotherhood

When somebody performs the Black Sacrament, the Brotherhood answers. Its
Sanctuaries are hidden, and each door asks a question that only a member can
answer.

*Playing here:* contracts, killers and the tenets they keep. Every hold
hangs a murderer.

## Those who hunt

### The Vigilants of Stendarr

The Vigilants hunt Daedra worshippers, necromancers and vampires, whoever
they offend in doing it. The Hall of the Vigilant burned in the last century
and has been rebuilt twice since.

*Playing here:* zealots with good reason. The Jarls tolerate them as long as
they ask first.

### The Dawnguard

Fort Dawnguard in the Rift was rebuilt when the vampires of Volkihar came
close to putting out the sun, and it is still kept because the vampires are
still there. The Dawnguard fight with crossbows and Dwarven bolts.

*Playing here:* vampire hunters. Their old enemy still holds its island.

### The Silver Hand

Werewolf hunters who no longer tell a beast from a man who might become one.
Most Jarls call them bandits, and the Companions call them worse. Their
grudge against Jorrvaskr is older than anybody alive.

*Playing here:* hunters on the wrong side of the law, certain they are right.

## Those who were here first, or came anyway

### The Forsworn

The Reach was theirs before Markarth was anybody's, and they have never
accepted otherwise. The Markarth Incident is history to everybody else and
yesterday to them. Ulfric hunted them for the rest of his life. High King
Eirik leaves the hills alone.

*Playing here:* Reachfolk with briarhearts and grudges. Outlaws in Markarth,
heroes in the hills.

### The Orc Strongholds

Dushnikh Yal, Mor Khazgur, Narzulbur and Largashbur keep the Code of
Malacath and keep to themselves. The Crown leaves them be and buys their
steel. A Chief rules each stronghold, and the strongholds speak with one
voice only when somebody threatens all of them.

*Playing here:* blood-kin, the Code, and forges older than the Moot.

### The Khajiit Caravans

Three caravans travel the roads between Whiterun, Markarth and Windhelm. The
cities still keep them outside the walls, and they trade at the gates. The
Accord protects Imperial merchants on Skyrim's roads, but it does not cover
traders from Elsweyr, so the caravans travel on each Jarl's leave. Moon sugar
and skooma are outlawed in every hold, and the guards search their wagons.

*Playing here:* traders, guards and drivers who travel the whole country and
hear what is said in every hold.

### The Blades

The last of the Emperor's dragonslayers keep Sky Haven Temple in the Reach.
They serve no Emperor now. The Dragonborn they served has passed into
legend, and the few dragons left are old. The Blades still keep the temple,
the wall of prophecy and the oaths of their order.

*Playing here:* a sworn order with a temple, a wall of prophecy and very few
recruits.

### The Volkihar Clan

The vampires of the castle on the island off Solitude, older than the war and
patient enough to outlast everything after it. They failed to blot out the
sun, but they kept the castle. Every hold's law kills a vampire on sight.

*Playing here:* vampires with a court of their own, hunted by the Dawnguard,
the Vigilants and every hold's guard.

## Joining

A faction's members and its head are given in Discord, as roles. A faction's
head is its Leader, applied for in character on the Faction Leader
application, and gives the faction's member role to the people they take in.
The faction's book in the game belongs to whoever holds its Leader role, and
moves with it. A faction not on this page is asked for as Other on that same
application.

Nobody on staff leads a faction or holds a Jarl's seat.
