# Fifty Years of a Free Skyrim

*Being an account of the years since the Crown was settled, set down at
Windhelm in the year 4E 252.*

## The War, and the end of it

By the close of 4E 202 the Legion held the road and nothing else. Solitude had
been taken in the winter. General Tullius was dead. The Empire, with the
Dominion at its throat and no strength left to spare for a province that had
stopped paying, signed.

The terms were short. Skyrim would govern Skyrim. Imperial soldiers would leave
by the first thaw. The Empire kept its claim on paper and nothing else.

Ulfric Stormcloak was crowned High King by the Moot at Windhelm in 4E 203. He
was fifty-one. He would hold the crown for twenty-six years.

## The Expulsion

The Thalmor did not leave when the Legion left. They had never been the
Empire's, only its guests, and they saw no reason a change of flag should
trouble them.

In 4E 209 the High King ordered them out. The holds call it the Expulsion. The
embassy at Haafingar was emptied in a night. Every Justiciar in Skyrim was
given until the new moon to be over a border, and those who were not were
hunted.

The Aldmeri Dominion protested. It did not march. It was still bleeding from a
war of its own, and Skyrim was no longer worth the cost of taking.

The Thalmor have been illegal in Skyrim for forty-three years, and some of them
are still here.

## The Sundered Years

The years after the Expulsion brought no peace, but nobody in Skyrim was
strong enough to make war.

Justiciars who had not made the border went to ground. Some died in it. Some
did not, and their grandchildren are still here, running gold and poison and
worse under other names, through the holds and along the coast. They have no
name for themselves. Other people call them the Remnant.

Most of them do not want Skyrim back. No Dominion order to retake it has
reached them, and no Dominion fleet has been seen off the coast. Their cause
has looked dead for forty years, and what they want now is coin, safety, and
revenge on whoever is nearest.

As far as anyone can tell they answer to nobody any more, and that makes them
the most dangerous people in Skyrim.

## The White Harbour Accord

The Empire could not come back with soldiers, so it came back with trade.

By 4E 218 the harvests had failed twice, the eastern roads were shut, and
Skyrim could not feed itself. Cyrodiil had grain. Skyrim had ore, timber, and a
coastline the Dominion had not tried to blockade.

The talking took three years and one walkout. The Accord was signed at Dawnstar
in 4E 221 and it is the reason there is an Imperial embassy in Solitude today.

The Accord is a trade, not an apology. Both sides got the thing they needed and
neither got the thing they wanted. The Empire wanted the province. It got a
warehouse. Skyrim wanted the Empire gone for good. It got grain.

The full text is kept at the Blue Palace and a copy hangs in every hold's
longhouse, because the High King of the day wanted every Nord able to read
exactly what had and had not been given away.

## The Free Skyrim Trading Company

Article the Third left the Empire one house in Skyrim, and the East Empire
Company was not it. In the spring of 4E 222 its factors at Solitude and
Windhelm sold what they could carry and walked away from what they could not.
The piers had never been theirs to sell. They went back to the holds that stood
over them, as Article the First said every harbour would.

What was left on the docks was the work of getting a crate from one hold to
another and being paid for it. The Free Skyrim Trading Company took it up. It
rents the old warehouses from the Jarls, owns no pier and no ship, and hires
both from whoever has them. It carries for anyone who pays, keeps its cut when
a load arrives, and buys the things that leave Skyrim for good. The one
warehouse the Empire kept is its embassy, and the Company does not rent it.

Nobody agrees who founded the Company or whose silver paid for its charter.
The signs over the old doors still say East Empire in places, and the Company
has not painted them over.

## Now

Ulfric died in his bed in 4E 229.

The Moot sat for eleven days and did not choose his kin. It chose **Eirik
Ravensgard** of the Rift, who had been a housecarl at nineteen and a Jarl's
steward at thirty and who had made no enemies worth the name. He has held the
crown for twenty-two years. He is neither loved nor hated.

The crown sits above the holds and below nothing. There is no Emperor over it.
Each hold keeps its own Jarl, its own court, its own law within the High King's
law, and its own opinion of everyone else's.

The roads are safer than they were, and still not safe. The mines are running.
There is an embassy in Solitude flying a flag that used to fly over all of
this. Its staff are polite, and few in Skyrim trust them.

The Remnant still keep their old orders, and nobody in Skyrim knows whether
anyone is left to give them new ones.

Plenty of people alive today remember the war.
