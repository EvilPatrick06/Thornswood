# Fifty Years of a Free Skyrim

The history of the fifty years since the Crown was settled is on the site, at
[thornswood.net/world](https://thornswood.net/world).

That page is the only copy. Its file is `site\data\site\world.md`: the
history first, then the White Harbour Accord, then what the Accord means for
the people living under it. Change the history there, not here.

This file used to carry a copy of its own. The two were edited apart: on
22 October the narrator's asides came out of both (#3345), and this copy and
the Accord beside it came out shorter than the page, with an older "what this
means" list that still said a Jarl's seat was found out in character after the
page said it is asked for on the Jarl application. So the copy went (#3138),
the way the rules' copy went from `game-rules.md` (#430).
