# lore

The world as the people in it know it: `history.md` is the fifty years since
the Crown was settled, `accord.md` is the treaty that puts an Imperial embassy
in a country that threw the Empire out, and `game-rules.md` points at how
roleplay works here, which is `site\data\site\rules.md` (the /rules page) and
nowhere else.

In-world writing, not operational text. That is why `scripts\dev\Check-Words.ps1`
reads the pages people are handed and leaves this folder alone: a page written
by a Nord in 4E 252 is allowed to sound like one. The public pages are
`site\data\site\*.md`, and those are checked.
