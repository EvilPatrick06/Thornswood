# lore

The world as the people in it know it. Each piece has one copy, and it is the
one people read:

- the fifty years since the Crown was settled, and the White Harbour Accord
  that puts an Imperial embassy in a country that threw the Empire out, are
  `site\data\site\world.md` (the /world page). `history.md` and `accord.md`
  here only point at it;
- how roleplay works here is `site\data\site\rules.md` (the /rules page), and
  `game-rules.md` points at it;
- `factions.md` is the factions.

The world page is in-world writing, not operational text, and it is held to
the same writing rules as every public page (`scripts\dev\Check-Words.ps1`
reads it). `factions.md` stays in this folder, which that check leaves alone:
a page written by a Nord in 4E 252 is allowed to sound like one.
