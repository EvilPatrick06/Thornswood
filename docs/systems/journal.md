# Journals

Your pages, on the character. Not a book in the world, and not a
quest log. Vanilla quests do not run.

## How many

12 journals. 60 pages in one journal. 4000 characters on a page. A
page title is 60 characters. Past that, the rest is cut off rather
than refused as a second page.

J opens the panel. `/journal` is the same. `/journal new` and a title
starts one.

The title, a page's title, and the page itself can be typed. While
the panel is open the keyboard stays in it, including in those
fields.

## Who else can read it

Nobody, until you share a page. You can share with a friend, and with
nobody else. See [Friends](friends.md). Sharing does not hand them
the journal. It hands them that page.

The panel's Share with list is your friends, including one who is
not online. The name stored with them is enough. If no friend is
picked, the panel says so and the share does not go out. A shared
page still only appears for them while you are online. When you
leave, it waits until you are back.

## Factions and businesses

J has a second tab for a group book: prices, members, and a chat for
when the group is not standing together. The tab is absent until the
Discord account holds Business Owner or Faction Owner, or the role
named Dev, or this character has accepted an invite, or the account
is staff. The book itself is on [Factions and businesses](factions.md).
It does not use one of the 12 journals. Someone with Prices stands
at that hold's notice board, which is the drop box, and submits a
copy. The list stays in the book.

## Where this is

The caps, the panel, typing in the title and the page, the commands,
and sharing with a friend are in the server. A friend who is offline
can be named, and the share is kept. The page still only appears
while the person who wrote it is online. A journal survives a
restart. It is not a quest. The group tab is on the same panel. A price list is submitted at
that hold's notice board, and the list stays in the book.
