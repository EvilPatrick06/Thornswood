# Tailor

Clothes, robes, and the dress of a faction.

- [Liverywright](tailor/liverywright.md). 102 recipes, all of them
  reach the server. Robes and faction dress.
- [Haberdasher](tailor/haberdasher.md). 231 recipes, 227 reach the
  server. Common and fine clothing. 4 are an armour table and do not.

The loom is open furniture. The garment's recipe is what refuses you.
The tanning rack is open, and leather from a hide is anybody's work.
The forge is open furniture too. A few tailor rows are made there.
Hair dye is a Tailor's work as well, either half, steeped at a cooking
spot: [Hair dye](../hair-dye.md).
The forge table names Liverywright at master and then marks the forge
open, so that master line is not a door. The recipe rank is the door.

## Master Weaver

At master. Clothing they make carries hidden armour, or keeps the
weather off, and still weighs what clothing weighs.

The tailor picks one per garment, warmth or armour, never both
(Patrick, 4 Oct). The pick is on K: a Master Weaver's own profession
card has Warmth and Armour under the perk, and the lit one is what
every garment they make from then on carries, until they press the
other. It starts on Warmth. Nothing is typed.

The weather half is 4 degrees of exposure ignored, on
[Temperature and weather](../temperature.md). It does not replace fur.
A garment woven for warmth comes out named Master-Woven, and wearing
one is what the warmth reads. One is enough. Two do not ignore 8.

The armour half gives back 10% of each hit taken while it is worn,
the way a Bulwark's Wall gives back its 20% (Patrick, 4 Oct). It is
not a warmth rule and not extra weight. A garment woven for armour
comes out named Master-Lined. One is enough: two give back 10%, not
20%. On a hit a Wall or a Cover also reaches, the two add, 30% back.
Like the Wall, a blow that kills outright is not softened, because
the engine applies it before anything can be given back.

The mark is the start of the garment's name, so a garment keeps it in
a trade and at any condition. A piece named that at the enchanting
table is not woven. A drop loses it for now: the engine places a
dropped piece without its name (#1719), and the garment comes back
without its mark until that is fixed in the fork.

## What you are handed

1 in Agility (+2 speed) and 1 in Efficiency (-3 spell cost, only in
schools you hold). The half places one more.

## Where this is

The recipe gates are in the server. Master Weaver is, both halves:
the pick on K, the weather half in the warmth, and the armour half on
the same path as the Wall. A dropped woven garment loses its mark
until #1719.
