## Race locks

Riekling 44, Dark Elf 8, Redguard 7, Wood Elf 4. Cultural dress, not
the metal tiers. The rows are in
[lists/race-locks.md](../../lists/race-locks.md).

A riekling's plain clothes are this half's novice work since 12
October 2026 (#614): the Riekling Belted Tunic and the Riekling
Footwraps, which a new riekling starts in. They are race locked, so a
riekling Haberdasher makes them, at the forge like the Riekling Fur
Cloak.

## What you are handed

The half places 1 in Agility. The profession already placed 1, so a
Haberdasher starts at +4 speed from 2 points. Five points fill Agility.

Your sister half is Liverywright at novice: 82 rows. Fine clothes past
novice on that list are not yours.

## Warmth

Cloth is 0.5 in the warmth table, linen and the light clothes. Fur is
1. A master weaver ignores 4 degrees of exposure and does not become
fur. The garment's material is still what the table says. See
[Temperature and weather](../../temperature.md).
