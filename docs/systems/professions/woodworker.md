# Woodworker

Fire, handles, bows, and instruments.

- [Charburner](woodworker/charburner.md). 5 recipes, all of them reach
  the server. The kiln is closed to everyone else.
- [Woodwright](woodworker/woodwright.md). 156 recipes. 86 reach the
  server. 70 are a sharpening wheel and do not.

The tool is a woodcutter's axe in the pack, 50 uses, handed when the
profession is picked. Chopping a block is this profession's work in
the world. The emote called Chop wood is an animation. It does not
fell a log and it does not count.

## Master Carver

At master. Fires and kilns they build or work are the hottest the
warmth rules allow: +48 at your feet, and still 0 at 300. An ordinary
fire is +34. See [Temperature and weather](../temperature.md).

The server cannot see who lit a fire. A fire counts as a Master
Carver's while one is working it: a master woodworker within reach of
a fire, and you within 300 of them in the same cell.

## What you are handed

1 in Endurance (+10 stamina) and 1 in Burden (+15 carry). The half
places one more.

## Where this is

The kiln, the axe, the chop, the recipe gates, and the hotter fire
are in the server. The axe has 50 uses.
