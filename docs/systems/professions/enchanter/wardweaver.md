# Wardweaver

Armour and clothing, enchanted. There is no recipe row. The arcane
enchanter is the bench, closed to anyone who is not an enchanter of
either half.

The work is the menu: learn an enchantment by breaking an item down,
then put it on a piece.

## What you are handed

The half places 1 in Ward. The profession already placed 1 in Ward,
so a Wardweaver starts at 2: +10 magic resistance.

You do not earn staff spell points. Bladebinder is your sister half
at novice: 66 staves, if you have the heart stone, and novice staff
spells only. A master Bladebinder can teach you further. That is an
apprenticeship inside the profession, not one of the two outside
trades.

## Twin Souls

At master this is the perk that matters here. Two enchantments on one
item. Below master, one.

## What you cannot do

You cannot bind a staff past the novice list without being taught.
You cannot put an enchantment on a person. Soul Trap on a person is
[Conjuration](../mage/conjuration.md) and [Death](../../death.md).

You have no item list to audit. If a mod later adds armour-enchant
recipes, they belong on this page and in a list beside the staves.

## Where this is

The bench gate is in the server. Twin Souls is the second slot in the
menu. An enchantment the server is told about is one piece of work.
Which enchantment it was is still the menu's: the engine does not
tell the server, so the list and its ranks wait on that (see
[Enchantments](../../enchantments.md)). The server does not see the
learned list.
