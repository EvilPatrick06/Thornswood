# Bulwark

The warrior half that holds the line. Shields, blocks, and staying
up. There is no recipe list. A Bulwark makes nothing a forge would
recognise.

## What the half places

1 in Resilience. That is +10 armour, on top of the warrior profession's
Vitality and Might. Five points fill Resilience and grant Unshakeable:
nothing staggers you while you block. The one placed point does not
fill it. See [Skill tree](../../skill-tree.md).

## What you can do

Block, and fight, and be credited for a kill that is not a person.
The kill counts on Bulwark, not on Berserker, when Bulwark is the half
you picked.

Your sister half is Berserker at novice. You have its placed point
only if you picked Berserker. You do not get both placed points by
owning the sister half at novice. Novice on the sister half is the
standing to be taught further, not a second stat line.

## What you cannot do

You cannot craft. Standing at a forge uses the smith rules, and you
fail them unless a master smith has taught you one of your two outside
trades.

You cannot be credited for killing a person.

The maneuvers for this half are Set, Plant, Cover, and Wall. The
numbers, and what the server does with each, are on
[Warrior](../warrior.md).

## Where this is

The half can be picked, the Resilience point lands, kills of world
creatures credit the half by how hard they were, and its four
maneuvers are in. Plant refuses every stagger, the ones your own game
plays as well as the server's. Halving what a block costs is not in:
a block costs no stamina here yet, so there is nothing to halve.
