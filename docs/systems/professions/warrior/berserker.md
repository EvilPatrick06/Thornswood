# Berserker

The warrior half that breaks a line. Two-handed swings, closing
distance, putting somebody down. There is no recipe list.

## What the half places

1 in Might. The warrior profession already placed 1 in Might, so a
Berserker starts at 2 in Might: +0.10 melee damage. Five fills it.
The perk at five is Devastating Blow. Two points is not five. See
[Skill tree](../../skill-tree.md).

## What you can do

Fight, and be credited for a kill that is not a person, on this half.

A Bulwark's sister-half novice does not hand you this Might point.
The point lands when Berserker is the half you picked.

## What you cannot do

You cannot craft on the strength of this half.

You cannot be credited for killing a person.

The maneuvers for this half are Rush, Heavy, Break, and Wreck. The
numbers are on [Warrior](../warrior.md). They are not in the server.

## Where this is

The half can be picked and the extra Might point lands. Nothing in
combat reads the word Berserker beyond the kill credit going to
whichever half you hold.
