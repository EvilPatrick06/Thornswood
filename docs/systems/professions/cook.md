# Cook

Dinners and desserts.

- [Hearthcook](cook/hearthcook.md) makes the meals. 112 recipes, every
  one of them reaches the server.
- [Confectioner](cook/confectioner.md) makes the desserts. 28 recipes,
  every one of them reaches the server.

The oven is closed. Either half, from novice. The cookpot is open
furniture, and the recipe is still Hearthcook's, so a confectioner
at the pot is making their sister half and stops at novice meals.
The grain mill is Hearthcook only.

## Grand Chef

At master, either half. Every batch yields two instead of one. The
craft is still one piece of work toward the next rank. Both portions
are the same quality.

## Burnt, plain and fine

A dish comes out burnt, plain or fine, more often fine and less often
burnt the higher the cook's rank; a master never burns one. The odds
and what each one does are on the [Food](../food.md) page.

## What you are handed

1 in Fortitude (+10% health recovery) and 1 in Endurance (+10
stamina). The half places one more.

## Food, once it is made

What the meal does in the body is [Food](../food.md), not this page.
A cooked meal is 25 hunger and one small help for 20 minutes. A feast
is 40 and the help doubled for half the time. Eating takes 8 seconds.
At 90 hunger or above, another meal sickens and the help does not
apply.

## Where this is

Both lists, the oven, the mill, the cookpot's recipe gate, Grand
Chef's second portion and how a cook comes out are in the server. The food effects
beyond the current hunger numbers are the plan on the food page.
