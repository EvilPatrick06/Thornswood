# Woodwright

Bows, arrows, instruments, and the long weapons that are wood as much
as metal. Spears, pikes and halberds are this half when the table
files them here.

156 recipes. 86 reach the server. 70 are the sharpening wheel and do
not.

Benches: forge 75, sharpening wheel 70, Dawnguard bench 10, skyforge 1.

The Dawnguard bench is closed: this half at advanced. The skyforge is
advanced as well, shared with the smiths. The sharpening wheel is
this half or Edgewright, from novice, and the 70 rows on it never
reach the server.

A recipe has no gold price. What a piece sells for is the price a
person posts.

## Race locks

Riekling 13, Imperial 4, Khajiit 2, Orc 2.

## What you are handed

The half places 1 in Precision (+2 critical chance). The game may
ignore it. Endurance and Burden from the profession still land.

A bow does not take a tool handle. A battleaxe does, when the row is
hafted, and that handle is Charburner novice. Your sister half is
those two novice kiln goods: Poor Charcoal and the handle. You do not
get Charcoal, the briquette, or coke without this half being
Charburner or a master teaching you.

## The axe

Chopping a block is the work in the world, the axe in the pack, 50
uses. One successful chop is one piece of work on this half. The
emote is not a chop.
