# Blightbrewer

Poisons. Harm in a bottle. The half places 1 in Potency (+5 spell
power, and only in schools you hold, so a Blightbrewer who is not
also a mage gets nothing from that point until they have a school).
See [Skill tree](../../skill-tree.md).

## What you can do

Stand at an alchemy lab and make whatever the ingredients share, from
novice. Your sister half, Physicker, is yours at novice in the same
lab. You do not get Physicker's Clarity point unless Physicker is the
half you picked.

A poison someone drinks is the sip on
[Potions and restoration](../../potions.md): thirst +5, the same as
any unlisted drink of that kind. It does not skip injury or disease.
A poison that would kill a person is [Death](../../death.md).

## What you cannot do

You cannot use a cookpot, a forge, or an enchanter's table on the
strength of this half.

You cannot name a poison the server does not have a row for and have
it counted as a recipe. There are zero alchemy recipes in the table.

Master Chemist, at master, is two bottles from one batch.

## Where this is

The lab accepts this half from novice. A batch is one piece of work.
Which poison it was is not checked yet, because the engine does not
tell the server the effect (see [Mixtures](../../mixtures.md)). The
ladder for this half is on [Alchemist](../alchemist.md).
