Benches: sharpening wheel 401, forge 295, skyforge 4.

The wheel is closed. Edgewright or Woodwright, from novice. A
platewright does not temper a blade unless this half is theirs or a
master has taught it. The temper still does not reach the server, so
the closed door and the missing gate are both true: the wrong person
can be turned away, and the right person's temper is not scored.

A recipe has no gold price. What a piece sells for is the price a
person posts.

## Race locks

Redguard 5, Riekling 3. Nothing else on this half. Elven and Orcish
weapons are not locked.

## Handles and charcoal

Swords, greatswords and daggers are a tang. They burn charcoal at a
forge and they do not take a tool handle.

War axes, battleaxes, warhammers, maces, and the other-weapon rows
take one tool handle as well as the charcoal. The handle is novice
charburner work, 1 firewood, 5 gold, and it was not in the ingredients
when the rank was chosen.

## What you are handed

The half places 1 in Precision (+2 critical chance). The game may
ignore that number. The smith profession's Might and Burden still
land. See [Skill tree](../../skill-tree.md).

A Platewright's sister-half right is your novice 54, not your
advanced 322.
