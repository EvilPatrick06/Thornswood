# Smith

Armour and weapons. The largest craft in the book.

- [Platewright](smith/platewright.md). 2198 recipes. 864 reach the
  server. 1334 are tempering and do not.
- [Edgewright](smith/edgewright.md). 700 recipes. 299 reach the
  server. 401 are the sharpening wheel and do not.

The forge is open furniture. The recipe's owner and rank are what
refuse you. The skyforge is not open: Edgewright, Platewright, or
Woodwright, at advanced. A novice smith cannot use it. The aetherium
forge is open furniture, and the things made there are master recipes,
so the rank still stops a novice.

Every forge and every skyforge burns one charcoal, cheapest in the
pack first. No charcoal, the piece is not made. Hafted weapons also
take one tool handle: war axes, battleaxes, warhammers, maces, and
the other-weapon rows. Swords, greatswords and daggers do not. The
handle is not in the ingredient list, so those rows were ranked as
if it were free. Both come from a
[Charburner](woodworker/charburner.md).

## Mastercraft

At master. Temper to legendary, past what the metal should take.
Tempering is the armour table and the sharpening wheel. Those rows
are in the lists and marked server asked: no. The perk is named.
It is not a gate.

## What you are handed

1 in Might (+0.05 melee) and 1 in Burden (+15 carry weight). The
half places one more.

## Where this is

The recipe gates, the skyforge's advanced line, the charcoal, and
the handle are in the server. Mastercraft is not applied to a
temper, because the temper never arrives.
