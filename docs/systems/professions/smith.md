# Smith

Armour and weapons. The largest craft in the book.

- [Platewright](smith/platewright.md). 2198 recipes. 864 reach the
  server. 1334 are tempering and do not.
- [Edgewright](smith/edgewright.md). 700 recipes. 299 reach the
  server. 401 are the sharpening wheel and do not.

The forge is open furniture. The recipe's owner and rank are what
refuse you. The skyforge is not open: Edgewright, Platewright, or
Woodwright, at advanced. A novice smith cannot use it. The aetherium
forge is open furniture, and the things made there are master recipes,
so the rank still stops a novice.

Every forge and every skyforge burns one charcoal. No charcoal of
the grade the metal asks for, the piece is not made. The four grades
are heat, not a discount. A higher grade burns hotter, and a higher
metal needs more heat. Poor charcoal, charcoal, briquette, and coke
are the grades a Charburner makes.

| Grade | Metals |
| --- | --- |
| Poor Charcoal | Iron, steel, ancient nord, skyforge steel |
| Charcoal | Silver, dwarven, elven, orcish, falmer |
| Briquette | Nordic, glass, ebony |
| Coke | Stalhrim |

Smelting an ingot uses the same grade as that metal. A skyforge asks
for one grade poorer than the table. Poor Charcoal does not get
poorer, so iron, steel, ancient nord, and skyforge steel still take
Poor Charcoal there. Stalhrim at the skyforge takes briquette.

Smelting an ingot is smith work, not anybody's. One row per ingot
stays. Leather is the Platewright's. The duplicate rows, and the
dragon and Daedric ingots, are in [Cut items](../cut-items.md).

A war axe, a battleaxe, a warhammer, and a mace are a metal head on a
wooden haft. Each of those recipes takes 1 Tool Handle, on top of the
ingots. The handle is novice Charburner work, 1 firewood, so it does
not raise the rank: the metal was already the dearest ingredient.
Swords, greatswords, and daggers are a tang and a grip. Bows and
staves are wood all the way through. None of those take a handle.

The pickaxe, the woodcutter's axe, and the shovel already name a
handle in their own records. They do not take a second one.

A drum, a flute, and a lute are filed in the same family as the odd
weapons, so the server currently asks them for a handle too. They
should not. An instrument is not a haft.

Repair is a kit, not a temper. See [Durability](../durability.md).

## Mastercraft

At master. The old line was temper to legendary, past what the metal
should take. That is the 120% repair now: an Edgewright's Weapon Kit
on a weapon, a Platewright's Armor Kit on armour. See
[Durability](../durability.md). The armour table and the sharpening
wheel are still in the lists and marked server asked: no.

## What you are handed

1 in Might (+0.05 melee) and 1 in Burden (+15 carry weight). The
half places one more.

## Where this is

The recipe gates, the skyforge's advanced line, the charcoal, and
the taking of one handle are in the server. The forge still burns
the cheapest grade in the pack, Poor Charcoal first, and it does not
read the table above. The ingredient column on
the recipe pages still omits the handle, because the plugin record
does. A drum, a flute, and a lute are asked for a handle by that same
check. Mastercraft is not applied to a temper, because the temper
never arrives.
