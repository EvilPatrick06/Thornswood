# Mage

No halves and no recipes. You pick two schools, then two starting
spells. The spells do not have to be one from each school. Both from
one school is a deep start. One from each is a wide start. You can
also spend neither point yet. The two points stay until you do.

At master you pick a third school, and you receive two more spell
points on top of the one that every other rank gave you.

The law of casting, the College, and what is refused are
[Magic and the College](../magic.md). This page is the profession.
The schools are the detailed book:

- [Destruction](mage/destruction.md)
- [Restoration](mage/restoration.md)
- [Alteration](mage/alteration.md)
- [Conjuration](mage/conjuration.md)
- [Illusion](mage/illusion.md)

Skyrim's Apprentice and Adept spells both sit under Advanced here.
Expert is Expert. Master is Master.

## Spell points

| When | Points |
| --- | --- |
| The profession is picked | 2 |
| Advanced | +1 |
| Expert | +1 |
| Master | +2, and the third school |

A mage who climbs with no College study holds 6 points by master.
Learning a spell spends one. A novice spell needs no spell below it.
Anything higher needs a spell of that school one rank down, and needs
your rank in that school to be at least the spell's rank.

Someone a master taught is not a mage. They hold 2 places, not a
mage's points: each spell takes a place and a point from a semester at
the College. They stay at novice, and they learn from the tome. That
cap is on the magic page.

## How the schools climb

Casting is not counted. Two things are.

A spell learned is 5 work in its school.

A rank gained anywhere else, including another school or a class, is
10 work in every school you hold. That 10 does not feed again, so a
raise cannot raise itself.

The count still resets at each rank: 100, then 250, then 600.

## What you are handed

The profession places 1 in Capacity (+10 magicka) and 1 in Clarity
(+10% magicka recovery). Each school you hold places one more, named
on that school's page. Two schools can land on the same sub-skill.
Clarity from the profession plus Clarity from Restoration is 2, not 1.

## Archmage

The master perk. The third school, and the standing to apprentice
people who are not mages. It is not the title Archmage of the College.
A title is given in the world. See [Offices](../offices.md) and
[Magic and the College](../magic.md).
