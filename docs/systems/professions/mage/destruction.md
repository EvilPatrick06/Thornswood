# Destruction

Fire, frost, and shock. The school places 1 in Potency when you take
it: +5 spell power, spread across the schools you actually hold. With
no school, Potency buys nothing. See [Skill tree](../../skill-tree.md)
and [Magic and the College](../../magic.md).

Your rank in Destruction is what caps the list. A point and a spell
one rank down are the other two gates. Apprentice and Adept both sit
under Advanced. No spell below the novice three. A new mage can spend
a starting point here.

## What this school is not

It is not a licence to kill. A bolt that would end a person is
[Death](../../death.md). The College does not punish it and does not
permit it either. The fight rules do.

It is not the only way to hurt somebody with a bottle. That is
[Blightbrewer](../alchemist/blightbrewer.md).
