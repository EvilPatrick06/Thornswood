Efficiency: -3 spell cost, in the schools you hold. With no school,
Efficiency buys nothing. See [Skill tree](../../skill-tree.md).

## What this school is not

Calm, Frenzy, Pacify, Harmony, Hysteria and Mayhem used on a person
are a fight. They do not rewrite what that person wants once the
spell ends, and they do not count as agreement. Ending the character
still needs the yes on [Death](../../death.md).

Invisibility is not a license to empty a house. Theft is the property
rule: take something, take a good thing, leave the rest.

Clairvoyance points at a quest the game knows. Most of those quests
do not run here, because the scripts do not run. The spell can be
learned. It often has nothing to point at.

Muffle does not change how far voice carries. Voice distances are on
[People beside you](../../people.md).
