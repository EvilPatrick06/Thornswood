## What this school is not

Paralyze and Mass Paralysis used to hold a person are a scene under
the rules: a moment of it is a fight, and hours of it need the yes
on [Death](../../death.md). The spell does not grant a cell.

Detect Life is not a license to read someone's pack, their gold, or
their journal. It is the spell the game already casts. The server
does not add a panel to it.

Waterbreathing does not change the temperature of the water. See
[Temperature and weather](../../temperature.md).
