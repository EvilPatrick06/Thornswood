# Restoration

Healing, wards, and turning the dead. The school places 1 in Clarity.
The mage profession also places 1 in Clarity, so a mage who takes
Restoration starts at 2 in Clarity: +20% magicka recovery. See
[Skill tree](../../skill-tree.md).

Healing a person and the strain of casting too often are on
[Potions and restoration](../../potions.md). This page is which spells
exist. That page is what repeated healing costs.

## What this school is not

A potion of cure disease is an item, not a spell. It is the sip on
[Disease](../../disease.md).

Healing does not clear an agreed loss, a crooked break, or a fever
by itself. See [Injury](../../injury.md).

Turn Undead and Bane of the Undead do not work on a person's
character. A vampire who was agreed to is a person. A draugr the
world placed is the spell.
