# Enchanter

Weapons and armour, as magic rather than as metal.

- [Bladebinder](enchanter/bladebinder.md) makes staves and unlocks the
  spells a staff can bind. 161 staves, all of them reach the server.
- [Wardweaver](enchanter/wardweaver.md) enchants armour and clothing.
  That is the enchanting menu. There is no recipe list.

The arcane enchanter is closed. Either half, from novice. The staff
enchanter is Bladebinder only, from novice. A Wardweaver does not
make a staff unless a master Bladebinder has taught them, and then
only novice staves, from the tome, two at most.

## Points for staff spells

Separate from a mage's spell points.

| When | Staff spell points |
| --- | --- |
| The profession is picked | 2 |
| Each Bladebinder rank before master | +1 |
| Master Bladebinder | +2 |

A Wardweaver does not earn these. They hold Bladebinder as the sister
half at novice until taught.

Unlocking asks for a point, a Bladebinder rank at least the spell's
rank, and a staff spell one rank down already unlocked. No tome. Press
K, Staff spells, Unlock. The spell is also put on the character,
because the crafting menu only lists a staff for somebody who knows
its spell.

Someone who is not an enchanter learns a staff spell only from its
tome, after a master Bladebinder teaches them, novice only, two at
most. That teaching is one of the two outside trades.

## Twin Souls

At master. Two enchantments on one item. The menu is one enchantment
today. The perk is named and not applied.

## What you are handed

1 in Capacity (+10 magicka) and 1 in Ward (+5 magic resistance). The
half places one more.

## Where this is

The bench gates, the staff list, the staff points, and the unlock
rules are in the server. Twin Souls is not. Soul gems and the
disenchant menu are the game's, and the server does not keep a list
of learned enchantments beyond the staff spells.
