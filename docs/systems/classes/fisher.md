# Fisher

A pole in the pack, then press E at the water. The fishing mod can
be replaced. Its scripts do not fire, and three poles plus fifteen
pieces of water are not a class.

The poles are remade as our own records when that mod goes: Crude,
Fine, and Master. A woodwright still makes them. The master pole
does not take dragon bone. Dragon bone is cut. What they take is
the table under The poles and the trap.

50 uses, the same as the pick, the axe, and the shovel. Crude fishes
novice water. Fine fishes novice through expert. Master fishes every
water. A pole under the water's rank is refused. When it breaks, a
woodwright makes the next one. A new fisher is handed a crude pole,
the same way a miner is handed a pick.

Salmon staying on eight spots, ten minutes apart, is on purpose.
Those eight are not the class. The other water that stands today is
three clams, one small pearl, one pearl, one horker, and one egg.
That is the whole of a new fisher's evening, and it is not enough to
climb.

The table under Every record is that current book. The same rows are
in [lists/fisher.md](../lists/fisher.md).

## The water this server places

Each of the nine mainland holds gets water on the river or the coast
it actually has. A hold with no sea does not grow a sea fish.
Solstheim does not get these counts. Solstheim stays scarce.

| Per mainland hold | Spots | What they are |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Novice | 12 | Slaughterfish meat and clam meat |
| Advanced | 6 | Histcarp, river betty, slaughterfish scales, small pearl |
| Expert | 3 | Silverside perch, Cyrodilic spadetail, pearl |

The eight salmon stay where they already stand, on top of the 12.
Abecean longfin is master, eight spots on the sea, scarce the way
salmon is scarce. The slaughterfish egg stays the one nest. The
horker stays the one piece on Solstheim.

A press from someone who is not a fisher does not take the water.
Once a spot is fished, it is fished for everyone.

A live slaughterfish is the hunter's. A cast that hands
slaughterfish meat is the fisher's. Nordic barnacle stays a
gatherer's plant.

## Between casts

A fish trap. A woodwright novice good, remade with the poles, not
taken from the fishing mod. Set it in water you are allowed to fish.
Up to 3 at once. The wait is that water's rank. Collecting it is a
catch of that water. Setting it and collecting it are each one piece
of work.

A cook turns the meat into a meal. The fisher does not.

## The poles and the trap

A woodwright makes them. The fishing mod's rows are not the plan.
The master pole does not take dragon bone. Dragon bone is cut.

| Piece | Rank | Asks for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crude pole | Novice | 2 firewood, 1 leather strips, 2 tundra cotton |
| Fine pole | Expert | 2 firewood, 1 leather strips, 1 steel ingot, 3 tundra cotton |
| Master pole | Master | 3 firewood, 2 leather strips, 1 steel ingot, 1 refined moonstone |
| Fish trap | Novice | 3 firewood, 2 leather strips, 1 iron ingot |

50 uses on a pole. A trap is not a pole. It is the trap above, up to
3 at once.

## How much, and how often

The wait is the spot's rank, not yours. It is kept on the water,
not on you: once a spot is fished, it is fished for everyone until
the wait is over. That holds for the water that stands today and for
the new water. A restart does not clear it, because return times and
shared stock survive a restart ([People who are not people](../npcs.md)).

| Spot's rank | Wait |
| --- | --- |
| Novice | 10 minutes |
| Advanced | 30 minutes |
| Expert | 1 hour |
| Master | 4 hours |

Salmon at 2 gold and a 10 minute wait is about 12 gold an hour off
one record, before the yield grows. There are many salmon records in
one stretch of river. The wait is what stops a pack of them.

What one press gives depends on your rank, least and most:

| Your rank | Pieces |
| --- | --- |
| Novice | 1 |
| Advanced | 1 to 2 |
| Expert | 1 to 3 |
| Master | 2 to 4 |

A novice takes the one the game would have given. Every rank above
takes more of the same thing. A novice cannot press the pearl or the
egg. The line tells them the rank.

One successful press is one piece of work, whatever the yield. 100,
then 250, then 600.

## The catch laid out in Windhelm

Fish laid out and hung up in Windhelm (three records in
WindhelmSSE.esp) are the fisher's. They are taken by hand, so no pole
is asked. They are novice, wait the novice water's 10 minutes, and give
the novice catch, one of whatever fish the record's own list holds.

## What you are handed

1 in Vigor (+10% stamina recovery).

## What you cannot do

You cannot run the fishing mod's minigame and have it count. The
cast is a press on water this server owns. You cannot dress a live
slaughterfish. If one is on the hunter list, that is the hunter's.
The egg nest is yours.

## Where this is

The fifteen records, the waits and the yields are in the server. The
wait is kept on the water, shared by everyone who fishes it and kept
through a restart (#1413), and a fished-out water says how long is left
in minutes and hours (#1826). Until 5 October this page said the wait
was kept per person and cleared by a restart, which it was before #1413
(#1887). A pole is required, and a new fisher is
handed a crude pole. The fishing mod's rods are in the collection and
do nothing, and its three pole recipes are cut (the cut file, #1766),
so the Woodwright's book holds only our three poles and the trap. None
of the new water is placed. The poles and the trap
are Woodwright recipes at the ranks above, in Thornswood-Content.esp
and the recipe table.
