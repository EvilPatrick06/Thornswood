# Fisher

Press E at the water. No rod, no net, and no fishing power. The rods
from the fishing mod run on scripts, and those scripts do not fire.
Six names, fifteen placed records.

The full table: [lists/fisher.md](../lists/fisher.md).

## How much, and how often

The wait is the spot's rank, not yours. It is kept in memory. A
restart clears it. The same is true of a picked plant.

| Spot's rank | Wait |
| --- | --- |
| Novice | 10 minutes |
| Advanced | 30 minutes |
| Expert | 1 hour |
| Master | 4 hours |

Salmon at 2 gold and a 10 minute wait is about 12 gold an hour off
one record, before the yield grows. There are many salmon records in
one stretch of river. The wait is what stops a pack of them.

What one press gives depends on your rank, least and most:

| Your rank | Pieces |
| --- | --- |
| Novice | 1 |
| Advanced | 1 to 2 |
| Expert | 1 to 3 |
| Master | 2 to 4 |

A novice takes the one the game would have given. Every rank above
takes more of the same thing. A novice cannot press the pearl or the
egg. The line tells them the rank.

One successful press is one piece of work, whatever the yield. 100,
then 250, then 600.

## What you are handed

1 in Vigor (+10% stamina recovery).

## What you cannot do

You cannot fish with a rod and have it count. You cannot dress a
slaughterfish carcass. If one is on the hunter list, that is the
hunter's. The egg nest is yours.

You do not need a tool.
