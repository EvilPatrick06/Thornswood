## Retirement design to review before implementation

1. Keep one authoritative private merchant ledger and its existing identity manifest. Separate that historical manifest from the 60 active interaction/population references; validate every historical reference/base against a committed source record. No inferred identity, deleted balance, redistribution or second initial grant.
2. Active merchants retain the existing actor/base/projection checks. A retired identity cannot open a panel or receive an initial purse. Its already-saved balance, stock, revision, supply history and pending intent remain intact in the same private ledger.
3. Recovery for a retired pending intent must operate on the validated historical ledger identity without requiring a spawned NPC or writing its native projection. Human identity, save receipts, inventory CAS and ledger revision checks remain mandatory. Retired state with no pending intent stays inaccessible to interaction.
4. Existing installations keep all 249 ledger keys. A genuinely new world grants a purse only to the 60 active merchants; inactive null entries receive no grant. Missing or corrupt initialized ledgers still stop startup rather than reset.
5. The generator applies the explicit active roster before writing population summaries, compact runtime data and the active merchant catalogue. Its historical merchant manifest remains deterministic. It must reject missing references, duplicate roles, wrong holds, mismatched bases and unresolved plugin identities.
6. Role item permissions apply to panel views, buying, selling and supplies on the server. Submitted hold prices remain authoritative; existing off-table stock remains saved but is not silently sold, deleted or reassigned.
7. Legacy open quests must have a reachable, explicitly documented turn-in through the retained roster before retiring their former interaction point. New quests bind to the selected merchant and tier; existing opaque quest fields and rewards remain preserved.

## Required regression boundaries

- Exactly ten holds, six different trades per hold, 60 unique active references and 189 retired references; every selected identity is currently kept and in the expected hold.
- A depleted active purse stays depleted; retired balances, stock metadata, supply history, revisions and pending intents survive restart and regeneration unchanged.
- Pending debits, credits and receipts recover with the retired NPC absent; refusal or uncertain outcomes never reset state, replay rewards or hand out another purse.
- Retired references and dynamic copies cannot trade through activation, typed commands or panel buttons.
- A first deployment grants only 60 purses; initialized missing/corrupt manifests remain fatal.
- Failure/reload during durable writes revokes the controller; old projections cannot overwrite the ledger.

## Independent review corrections and cut gates

The independent plan review found seven missing contracts. These are required before activating the roster, not completed implementation claims:

- One purse controller and one serialized private-ledger writer serve active trading and historical recovery together. There is no second retirement writer or copied ledger. Startup revokes the prior trade and purse controllers before loading the replacement. A stale controller cannot commit on either the same merchant or another merchant. Add namespace ownership and interleaved-write regressions before activating history in runtime data.
- One durable pending reservation excludes fresh merchant business on that merchant and all competing inventory work for that human. No supply cleanup, roster update or direct purse mutation may remove or replace a saved intent. Retirement only changes interaction permission; an existing intent remains discoverable until its exact saved final receipt completes it.
- Recovery for retained AND retired identities uses the original saved quantities, price, debit, credit and cooldown, even if an item is outside the new role table. New role checks apply before a fresh reservation; they never renegotiate or reject an already authorized saved intent. This exception grants no permission for fresh off-table trades.
- The linearization point is the acknowledged private intent write, before human inventory CAS. Changing roster generation revokes old interaction/admission first. A request without a durable intent is refused before charging; every durable intent remains in pendingRecords. Test reload/retirement at intent save, human CAS, debit acknowledgement, credit save and ledger completion.
- Historical recovery preflights the whole manifest: intent version, namespace, enclosing reference/base, human identity, transaction ID, receipt sequence, debit/credit and previous/next state must be valid. Duplicate pending humans, transaction IDs or receipt ownership across merchants are quarantined before any recovery write. Per-merchant revision checks alone do not establish uniqueness.

An explicit offline new-world initializer creates the initial manifest once under a separately recorded installation/world authorization; it is not part of restart, repair, Scan/Fix or routine installation. An existing world with lost ledger evidence stops for recovery. Document and test the actual initializer before adding any Claude command for it.
- Legacy accepted work uses the routes below and its original gates, progress and reward terms. New tier/role gates apply to new offers only. No old record is erased or silently reissued. The thirteen currently unbound offers remain unbound until their sources and recovery are implemented; opaque saved fields are preserved and never treated as permission to mint rewards.
