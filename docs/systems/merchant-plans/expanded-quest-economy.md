## Work mix to author

Use distinct finite-purse commissions for suitable gathering and craft work, and source-bound kill/retrieval scenes when a trade or specialization calls for another deed. A commission can request an authored bundle, with actual different goods and purpose by hold, rather than repeat one item under different names. Include only verified goods and valid producers at the intended difficulty. A new profession gate does not prove that the named half can produce a requested output; check actual recipe/resource evidence too.

Commissions are a new explicit contract type, not a silent widening of legacy supply. Preserve the existing five-good/six-hour dynamic supply rules and buy/sell/supply permissions. Any commission-specific permission must be source-derived and compiled as its own authority, then checked at display and final settlement. Do not infer supply permission from ordinary buy. Public outputs, ingredients and approved resources still need a submitted hold price. Missing price, unsupported goods or insufficient unreserved purse refuses new acceptance. The customer can accept before collecting goods. At delivery require matching inventory; a shortage leaves the work and reserved payment intact. Preserve the accepted quote rather than substituting a changed price or requiring fresh unreserved purse money. Ordinary goods for sale remain visible.

Pay commissions from the merchant's existing purse at the current submitted prices, through the existing durable pending-transfer and native inventory-receipt protocol. Never mint a separate fixed bonus, refill the purse or reset retired state. Multi-good bundles need explicit new conservation validation, exact metadata-preserving debits, stock updates and saved original terms. Existing single-good and saved legacy transfers retain their old shape and recovery. A commission label cannot be passed as an existing supply kind to bypass its validator. Changing a quote/session during a callback refuses the old request.

The first data slice in PR2721 contains kill scenes only; it does not implement any commission, new permission or transaction kind. The current source test deliberately rejects other kinds until their own exact validators and source evidence exist.

## Proposed new-scene allowance

Choose a shared ceiling of150 gold per character in any half-open rolling24-hour window for newly offered expanded kill/retrieval work, rather than a separate ceiling at every merchant. This is a proposed design value, not an active allowance or a claim of economy acceptance. It is about14 percent of the six existing kill rows' cooldown-only bound; that comparison alone does not prove balance. Complete catalogue quantities, rewards and cadence still require a full enumeration and conservative source budget check. Commissions transfer existing gold and do not spend this mint allowance.

Each new scene has an immutable accepted reward of at least10 and at most150 gold. Reserve its full reward at acceptance. Admission requires completed new-scene payouts inside the previous24 hours plus all outstanding new-scene reservations plus the new reward to be at most150. On completion, move the exact reservation into a timestamped payout entry while removing the quest item or crediting the verified kill result and adding the original gold in the same native inventory/property CAS. On cancellation, release that reservation without payout or a completion cooldown. An accepted reservation never expires merely because somebody takes a long time to finish. A change of profession or merchant must not release or duplicate it.

This avoids admitting work with an immediate promised payment and then refusing its turn-in because other contracts consumed the allowance. It also makes the final mint accounting and quest/inventory change one atomic actor transaction. No separate private budget write is used for scene rewards. A crash, restart or publication failure follows the existing receipt/property outcome rules; no compensating grant or replay based on a message being lost.

At minimum10 gold per payout, at most15 completed entries can occupy an active150-gold window. The reservation sum is bounded by the same allowance. This bounds that specific accounting list, not the full quest property. Use captured trusted server time, test exact boundary behavior and refuse invalid or backward time without erasing evidence. Prune only owned, demonstrably expired new-scene payout entries. Do not prune unknown/legacy records or target history as an indirect size fix. Old accepted work keeps original terms; any newly connected legacy-unbound offer needs an explicit classification/admission rule before activation, without retroactively charging an old accepted record.

The proposed150-gold bound is per character. It is not a world-wide or account-wide bound and must not be described as one. Character recreation or multiple characters are a separate rate dimension to quantify from actual allowed character creation and account identity before accepting the final economy budget. Do not invent current world balances or an account limit from a configured online seat count.

Source-only account inspection: site/server.js slotsFor gives two menu slots, or three for configured roles/admin/dev, while launcher Chars.Prepare uses the site's answer. The linked-account route keeps recently checked-in profile IDs and permits a new unclaimed ID; menu slots do not establish a lifetime creation allowance or a trusted creation-rate cap. Therefore the supported arithmetic is at most150 new scene gold per qualifying character per rolling24h, plus an additional allowance for each eligible newly created character. No finite account/world mint bound was proved by these reads. Activation still requires an explicit rate/exposure decision; do not quietly multiply150 by menu slots and call that an account cap.

## Persistence and integration gates

Keep scene reservation, payout history, accepted original terms, progress and new cooldowns in the same actor property used by the atomic quest transaction. Do not split them across independently written properties. Commissions instead need their explicitly versioned private-ledger cooldown/contract representation to participate in the durable merchant protocol; ordinary supply's six-hour pruning cannot safely hold arbitrary longer commission waits. Preserve pending transfers and unknown fields. Anvil's separate merchant release work in PR2663 owns deleted-character ledger lifecycle; inspect its merged interface before integrating new commission fields rather than taking over that lane.

Before runtime work, specify numerical bounds for simultaneous accepted scenes, per-objective target evidence, immutable catalogue identity and exact widths. Prove the complete representative serialized record, original terms and replay protection fit the native limit, including valid retained legacy data. A capacity refusal may prevent new acceptance; it must not erase existing work or goods. The new-scene allowance does not solve this full capacity problem, nor does a short ID by itself.

Adversarial reviews must inspect the acceptance/reservation/completion protocol, original-term recovery and rate dimensions before this proposal stands. Tests must include simultaneous competing completions, reentrant session/body/progression changes, cancellation, process recreation, expired-boundary payouts, malformed/missing reservations, unavailable native writes and uncertain publication. Test all new commission failure stages through exact saved-transfer conservation, receipt and purse/stock rules. No code, roster activation, plugin output, deployment, install or playtest is authorized by this document.
