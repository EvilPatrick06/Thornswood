# Custom quests

Quests this server runs. Papyrus quests do not run, so a vanilla
quest journal does not open. The offer, the turn-in, and the unlock
are the server's.

A trader of that hold's [Merchant's Guild](merchants-guild.md) gives
them. A steward does not. Staff do not. The traders are the actors
on [People who are not people](npcs.md). You speak to the trader.
The server accepts the goods, because the actor's own scripts do
not run.

## What they are for

A supply quest moves goods. The trader asks for a count of an item
that hold already buys. You bring it. The trader pays that hold's
buy price for the count, out of the trader's own purse. If the purse
cannot cover it, the trader does not offer the quest. If no trader
buys the item, there is no supply quest for it. Prices are on
[Merchant's Guild](merchants-guild.md).

A scarce quest opens a place that is otherwise shut. The trader asks
for a count of the scarce item. The place holds more than that count.
You may take the asked amount and the extra. You turn in the asked
amount. The extra stays in your pack. Then the place shuts for you
until the cooldown ends.

Until the turn-in, the extra is not yours. Walking away without it
puts the goods back and shuts the place. The cooldown does not start.
You cannot take more from the place while the quest is unfinished.

The rare mine is this second kind. Ebony, the gems, stalhrim, and
heart stone are one place each. See [Miner](classes/miner.md). The
rank on the seam still applies.

A scene quest is for the evening. It is not stock for the shop and
it does not open a mine. The trader asks you to do a thing in the
world: carry a letter, repeat what you were told, walk to a place,
sit an hour, stand a watch, bring meals to a sitting. The gold is
the amount written on that quest. It is created when you finish.
It does not come out of the trader's 250, and it does not run out.
The same cooldown rules apply. These six are open to anyone.

| Quest | What you do | Gold | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The letter | Carry their letter, sealed, to the trader in a hold they name. You do not open it. | 15 | 2 hours |
| The telling | Hear what they tell you about this hold, and say it in your own words to the trader in another hold. | 15 | 4 hours |
| The road | Go to a place they name in this hold, then come back and say what is there. | 20 | 4 hours |
| The sitting | Spend an hour at the inn they name, then come back. | 20 | 6 hours |
| The table | Hand them three meals from your pack, for a sitting. The meals leave your pack. | 25 | 6 hours |
| The watch | Stand at a door they name for an hour, in the world, then come back. | 25 | 8 hours |

A richer one waits longer. Another scene can be added the same way:
a set amount of gold, and its own cooldown.

## Who may take one

The quest names the gates. It may require a class, a profession or
one half of it, and a race. A line it leaves blank is open. A quest
that names none of them is open to anyone who can speak to that
trader.

One copy at a time. You cannot take it again while it is open, and
you cannot take it again during the cooldown. The cooldown is yours.
Another person can hold the same quest.

## The cooldown

Each quest has its own cooldown. It starts when you turn the goods
in. A richer reward waits longer. The hours are written on that
quest. There is not one wait for every quest.

## Where this is

No custom quest exists. Traders do not offer work. The rare places
are still the several veins the server has today. A scene reward is
not created. Papyrus quests do not run.
