# Friends

Someone you have both agreed to know. A friend is not a party, and a
friend does not grant the work bonus. That bonus is
[Party](party.md).

## How it is made

Look at them and press Y, or `/friend` and their name. A prompt
appears on their screen with Accept and Refuse. They do not type a
command to answer. The offer stands for 45 seconds. Both sides are
written. A list with one name in it is not a friendship.

`/friend` with nothing after it lists them, and says who is away.
`/friend remove` and the name takes one off. P opens the same list.

Two people with the same name are an ambiguity. The key while you are
looking at them is exact. A typed name is a prefix.

## How many

120. That is the size of the list the server sends to the client, not
a social rule somebody asked for. The 121st accept is refused.

## What a friend is for

You can share a journal page with a friend, and with nobody else. See
[Journals](journal.md).

A friend is not let into your house. A key does that. See
[Property](property.md).

A friend is not a mute. What you can turn off for one person is on
[Voice](voice.md): their voice, their text, or their name. Each one
is only for you. They can still hear you, still read you, and still
see your name.

## Where this is

Y, `/friend`, the prompt, the 45 seconds, the list, the remove, and
the cap of 120 are in the server. Accept and Refuse on that prompt
are what a person presses. The list survives a restart.
