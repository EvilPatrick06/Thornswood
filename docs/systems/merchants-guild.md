# Merchant's Guild

The shops of one hold. It is a roll and a ledger. It is not a
profession. A merchant has no verb and no recipes.

Each hold has its own guild. Joining Whiterun does nothing for a
notice in Riften.

## Joining

At that hold's board, `/guild join`. 50 gold, taken the way a notice
fee is taken, onto that hold's ledger. You do not need a shop, a
class, or a profession.

The 50 is not returned if you leave or if you are struck.

`/guild` at the board reads the roll: who is in good standing, and who
is behind on dues.

## Dues

10 gold per in-game week, starting the week you join.

Skyrim's clock runs at 20. One day in the world is 72 minutes of real
time. One week is 8 hours 24 minutes.

The server counts the debt. It does not pull the gold. You put it in
the hold's box, or you hand it to the factor. Gold in the box pays
what you owe in this order: rent, then guild dues, then the rest is
the hold's. See [Property](property.md).

While you owe nothing, your notices in that hold are 10 gold and your
jobs are 15. While you owe anything, you pay the full 20 and 30.

Two unpaid weeks do not remove you. They only keep the half-fee off
until the debt is cleared. Paying the whole debt puts the half-fee
back. A missed week is still owed. Paying one week of a two-week debt
leaves you behind.

## The factor

One factor a hold, named by that hold's steward, the same way a jarl
names a steward. The word refuses a person who is not a member.

The factor can strike a name, and can confirm a company delivery when
the steward is not there. See
[Free Skyrim Trading Company](trading-company.md).

Naming a new factor replaces the old one.

## A strike

The steward or the factor, at the board: `/guild strike` and the name.
The name leaves the roll. Half-fees stop. Joining again costs another
50, and the old debt does not carry if they were struck rather than
merely behind. A person who is only behind is still on the roll, and
striking them is how the debt is dropped.

## What the guild does not do

It does not set prices. Two people trade, and the gold moves as
[Gold](gold.md) says.

It does not mint coin. Joining and dues are gold that already existed,
counted on the hold's box.

It does not own a shop. The shop is a claim. See [Property](property.md).

It does not stop anyone else from posting or selling. A non-member
pays full fees and trades the same way.

It does not pay a wage.

## Where this is

The hold's box is in the server, and the note beside it says guild
ledgers belong in that box. There is no roll, no 50, no dues, no
factor, and no half-fee. Posting is 20 and 30 for everybody. Rent is
the only debt the box pays down.
