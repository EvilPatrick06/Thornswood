# Voice

How far a spoken word carries. The launcher holds the microphone.
The game does not. Hold V to talk. O leaves the microphone open. B
steps the distance.

The launcher has to be open. Muffle and Invisibility do not change
these distances.

| Voice | Full strength out to | Gone at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whisper | 1.5 metres | 7 metres |
| Ordinary | 4 metres | 30 metres |
| Yell | 9 metres | 85 metres |

A yell is louder than a whisper at the same spot. Past the far
number, the level is nothing.

## Mute

Look at them and press L, or `/mute`. You stop hearing them. It does
not stop them hearing you. It is not a staff silence. `/mute` with
nothing after it lists who you have muted.

A mute is saved on the character. It survives a restart.

## Where this is

The three distances, V, O, B, and mute are in the server. The
microphone is the launcher's.
