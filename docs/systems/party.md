# Party

Who you are working beside this evening. It is not a friendship and
it does not survive a restart. Friends are [Friends](friends.md).

## How it is made

Look at them and press P. Under somebody who is not in your party,
the look prompt says "Ask to party" beside P, and P sends the offer.
Ask to party beside a friend who is online, on the P panel, sends it
too. It goes to whoever that friend is playing now, a new character
under a new name included, and when they are not in the world it
says so and asks nobody. A prompt appears on their screen with
Accept and Refuse, the same prompt a friend request and a trade
offer use. They do not type a command to answer. The offer stands
for 45 seconds.

P with nobody in front of you, or with somebody from your party
there, opens the P panel. It lists who is in the party and how many
of them are in the cell with you. Leave on it drops you. Nothing
about a party is typed in the chat.

Six, including you. A seventh accept is refused. Six is enough for a
scene and small enough that the bonus below does not become a second
ladder.

A party is held in memory. When the server comes back up, the party
is gone. Friends, mutes, and journals are not.

## The bonus

Work done while somebody from your party is in the same cell banks
15% of that piece. At a whole piece it pays one extra. That is every
seventh piece when each piece is worth 1. A kill that is worth 4
banks 0.6 of a piece.

Being on the list and standing in another hold pays nothing.

The bonus applies to every trade that already goes through the work
counter: a seam, a log, a fish, a craft, a kill that counts.

## The leader

The person who opened the party is the leader. If they leave without
handing the lead across, the lead passes to the next person still on
the list. A party of one ends.

Anyone in the party can invite. Anyone can leave.

Only the leader can do these three, with the buttons on P:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Kick, beside a name | That person leaves. If two or more remain, the party stays. |
| Lead, beside a name | That person becomes the leader. They have to be in the party. |
| Disband | The party ends for everyone in it. |

On P, the leader sees Kick and Lead beside each other person, and
Disband for the party. Everyone, the leader too, sees Leave.

## Finding them

A party member's name is green above their head. They have a marker
on your map, and an arrow on the strip under the compass with how far
away they are. Green on the strip means the same room. Gold means
somewhere else. The strip is this server's, not Skyrim's compass.

Hiding their name, on [Voice](voice.md), hides the green name. The
marker and the arrow stay.

A speaking mark uses the colors on [Voice](voice.md), and it sits
above the green name.

## Where this is

The offer, the prompt, the leave, the cap of 6, the 15%, and the
same-cell test are in the server. P on somebody not in your party
sends the offer, and P on a party member or on nobody opens the
panel. Ask to party beside a friend sends the server that friend's
profile id, the one the friends list is kept by, never a name, and
the server asks whoever that profile is playing now. Accept and
Refuse on that prompt are what a person presses. Anyone in the party
can already invite. The bonus is paid as a whole extra piece, not as
a fraction on the screen.

The leader is stored. P shows Kick, Lead, and Disband to the leader,
and Leave to everyone. The server answers the lines those buttons
and the P key send, and no help names them. If the leader leaves,
the lead already passes, and a party of one already ends. The green
name, the map marker, and the strip are in the server. The speaking
mark sits above that green name while the microphone is open, and it
does not recolor it.
