# Party

Who you are working beside this evening. It is not a friendship and
it does not survive a restart. Friends are [Friends](friends.md).

## How it is made

Look at them and offer, or `/party` and their name. They accept. The
offer stands for 45 seconds. `/party` lists who is in it and how many
of them are in the cell with you. `/party leave` drops you. P opens
the same list.

Six, including you. A seventh accept is refused. Six is enough for a
scene and small enough that the bonus below does not become a second
ladder.

A party is held in memory. When the server comes back up, the party
is gone. Friends, mutes, and journals are not.

## The bonus

Work done while somebody from your party is in the same cell banks
15% of that piece. At a whole piece it pays one extra. That is every
seventh piece when each piece is worth 1. A kill that is worth 4
banks 0.6 of a piece.

Being on the list and standing in another hold pays nothing.

The bonus applies to every trade that already goes through the work
counter: a seam, a log, a fish, a craft, a kill that counts.

## The leader

The person who opened the party is the leader. If they leave without
handing the lead across, the lead passes to the next person still on
the list. A party of one ends.

Anyone in the party can invite. Anyone can leave.

Only the leader can do these three:

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/party kick` and the name | That person leaves. If two or more remain, the party stays. |
| `/party lead` and the name | That person becomes the leader. They have to be in the party. |
| `/party disband` | The party ends for everyone in it. |

On P, the leader sees Kick and Lead beside each other person, and
Disband for the party. Everyone else sees Leave.

## Finding them

A party member's name is green above their head. They have a marker
on your map, and an arrow on the strip under the compass with how far
away they are. Green on the strip means the same room. Gold means
somewhere else. The strip is this server's, not Skyrim's compass.

Hiding their name, on [Voice](voice.md), hides the green name. The
marker and the arrow stay.

A speaking mark uses the colors on [Voice](voice.md), and it sits
above the green name.

## Where this is

The offer, the accept, the leave, the cap of 6, the 15%, and the
same-cell test are in the server. Anyone in the party can already
invite. The bonus is paid as a whole extra piece, not as a fraction
on the screen.

The leader is stored, and P tags them. If the leader leaves, the lead
already passes, and a party of one already ends. The green name, the
map marker, and the strip are in the server. Kick, handing the lead
across, and disband are not commands. P offers Leave to everyone in
the party, including the leader.
