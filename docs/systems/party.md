# Party

Who you are working beside this evening. It is not a friendship and
it does not survive a restart. Friends are [Friends](friends.md).

## How it is made

Look at them and offer, or `/party` and their name. They accept. The
offer stands for 45 seconds. `/party` lists who is in it and how many
of them are in the cell with you. `/party leave` drops you. P opens
the same list.

Six, including you. A seventh accept is refused. Six is enough for a
scene and small enough that the bonus below does not become a second
ladder.

A party is held in memory. When the server comes back up, the party
is gone. Friends, mutes, and journals are not.

## The bonus

Work done while somebody from your party is in the same cell banks
15% of that piece. At a whole piece it pays one extra. That is every
seventh piece when each piece is worth 1. A kill that is worth 4
banks 0.6 of a piece.

Being on the list and standing in another hold pays nothing.

The bonus applies to every trade that already goes through the work
counter: a seam, a log, a fish, a craft, a kill that counts.

## Where this is

The offer, the accept, the leave, the cap of 6, the 15%, and the
same-cell test are in the server. The bonus is paid as a whole extra
piece, not as a fraction on the screen.
