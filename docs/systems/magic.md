# Magic and the College

What a person may cast, where they learn it, and what the College of
Winterhold is for. The profession is [Mage](professions/mage.md). The
spells, school by school, are linked from that page. This page is the
rule around them.

Enchanting is not this profession. It is
[Enchanter](professions/enchanter.md). Alchemy is
[Alchemist](professions/alchemist.md). A staff is made by a
Bladebinder and casts a spell the enchanter has unlocked. The spell
still has to be in this book.

## Who may cast

A mage casts the spells they know, in the schools they hold, up to
their rank in that school.

Anybody else casts nothing until a master mage apprentices them. Then
they hold 2 spells at most, novice, one school, learned from the tome.
Those 2 places never become a third school and never climb past novice.
That teaching is one of the two outside trades. The whole arrangement,
including a sister half that is not magic, is
[Masters and apprentices](apprenticeship.md).

There is no licence, and the College does not sell one. Magic in a
hold street is legal. Destruction that hits a person is a fight under
[Death](death.md), not a college crime. The Thalmor do not police
spells. They are outlaws, and Talos is lawful. See
[The Thalmor](thalmor.md).

## Learning a spell

Three gates, all of them, whether you pick it on K or read a tome:

1. A spell point. A mage earns them by rank: 2 to start, +1 at
   advanced, +1 at expert, +2 at master. Anybody else has only the
   2 places a master gave them.
2. Your rank in that school is at least the spell's rank. Apprentice
   and Adept are both Advanced.
3. You already know a spell of that school one rank down. A novice
   spell needs none.

Miss one and nothing is spent. You keep the tome. The line says which
gate failed.

A master spell picked on K needs the tome as well. The tome for that
spell has to be in the pack, the rank in that school has to be master,
and a spell point has to be there. The tome leaves the pack when the
spell is learned. Reading the tome is the other door. That book is
already in hand, so reading it does not ask for a second copy. The
spell one rank below is still required.

A spell learned is 5 work in its school. Casting the spell afterwards
is not work and is not a rank.

## A semester

A semester is 24 hours of real time.

Stand in the College, press K, open College and press Study. The
rooms that count are the Hall of the Elements, the courtyard, the Hall
of Countenance, the Hall of Attainment, and the Arcanaeum. Anywhere
else refuses. Nothing is typed.

A mage receives 2 spell points.

A person a master has taught receives 1, and it can only be spent on a
novice spell of the school they were taught.

Study gives nothing again until 24 hours have passed. Logging out
does not reset it. The College does not have to be held by you. The
doors follow [Property](property.md): if the Archmage's office holds
the halls, you need to be let in.

## The College

Winterhold. It answers to its Archmage. That is a title, given or
taken in the world, not the master perk of the same word. A master
mage is not the Archmage because they hit the rank. See
[Offices](offices.md).

The College does not set prices, mint coin, or grant a profession.
Study is the only thing the building adds to the numbers.

The halls are claimable buildings. A claim on them is the College's
problem and the court's, the same as any other hall.

## What you cannot do

You cannot cast a spell that is not in the five schools on the mage
page. A mod that adds a spell, Apocalypse among them, is not in the
book. It is not a college spell and it is not granted.

You cannot learn a third school before master.

You cannot shout. The Dragonborn, the greybeards' way, and a free
shout from a word wall are a legend, and the rules already refuse
that life.

You cannot turn a corpse that is a person's character into a thrall.
Dead Thrall, Revenant, and the reanimate spells used on a person are
a death or a permanent change, and they need the yes on
[Death](death.md). A body the world placed is the spell working as
written.

You cannot buy a rank, a point, or a school with gold.

Vampirism and lycanthropy are not college magic. They need the same
yes as a death.

## Where this is

The book, the points, the tomes, and the three gates are in the
server. A dungeon chest can put a novice or a master tome in the pack.
Reading it still has to pass the three gates. Picking a master spell
on K spends the tome. See [Dungeon loot](dungeon-loot.md).

A semester is in the server. The College page on K has the Study
button. The server checks the room: the four halls by their cells, and
the courtyard as the open ground the halls' outside doors stand in. A
mage receives 2 points and a person a master has taught receives 1.
The moment is kept on the character, so logging out and a restart do
not reset the 24 hours.

A person a master has taught needs a semester's point as well as one
of their 2 places to learn a spell from a tome, because the semester is
the only thing that hands them a point. Whether the places alone should
be enough is asked on issue 1387.

Only the book is granted. A tome of a spell the book does not list is
refused, and the tome is kept. A spell like that which a tome taught
before this comes off the character at the next login, and a mage has
the point back. A spell a mod's menu hands out is taken back the moment
it is put in a hand, and the client drops it at every spawn as well. A
disease, a power, an ability, a shrine blessing, the instruments, and a
staff spell the staff rules unlocked are not spells of the book and
stay.

A tome whose spell has no school at all is refused the same way, with
the same line, and the tome is kept: the Oghma Infinium, which a mod
makes teach Dragon Knowledge, and the fishing mod's four cheat books.

The server refuses Dead Thrall and every spell that raises the dead on
a person's body. See [Conjuration](professions/mage/conjuration.md).
