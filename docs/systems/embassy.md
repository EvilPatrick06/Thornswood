# The Imperial Embassy

One house, in Solitude. Article the Third of the Accord. The Empire
wanted the province and got a warehouse. This is that warehouse, and
it is a place with a door, a roll, and a law that is not Skyrim's
for the people standing inside it.

The text of the Accord is on the site. This page is the numbers.

## The roll

40 persons in service, no more. Of those, 12 may be marked armed, and
those 12 bear arms inside the walls only.

The Ambassador keeps the roll. The Ambassador is a title, not a class.
See [Offices](offices.md). Naming a 41st person is refused. Marking a
13th as armed is refused.

Armed means the roll says so. The server does not strip a sword at
the gate by looking at the pack. Outside the gate, a person the roll
marks armed has broken the Accord, and the Ambassador hands them to
the Jarl of Haafingar rather than shelter them. That handoff is a
scene. It is not a teleport.

A veteran who served decades ago and settled in a hold is not on this
roll and is not limited by the 12.

## The law at the door

Inside the walls, a citizen of Skyrim stands under Imperial law, and
is owed it. They cannot be held, cannot be questioned without a
witness of their own choosing, and cannot be tried for anything.

Outside, an Imperial citizen stands under Skyrim's law. The embassy
is not a border. A robbery in Solitude is a robbery in Solitude if
they run through the gate. The Ambassador hands them out, or answers
for not doing it.

Neither side reaches in and takes a person. The Empire asks the Jarl.
Skyrim asks the Ambassador. Both may say no. Saying no is not a war
by itself. Ending the Accord is a declaration at Dawnstar, in person,
before witnesses of both, and everything in it stands for a year and
a day after. That declaration is an event, not a command.

## The building

The embassy doors start held by the Ambassador's office. `/claim` on
them answers that the embassy is held. A steward of Haafingar does not
evict the Accord. A Thalmor does not found a claim on it. See
[The Thalmor](thalmor.md) and [Property](property.md).

It is not a hold. It has no notice board of its own, no box, and no
mint. Solitude's board and Solitude's box are the hold's. Rent inside
the embassy, if the Ambassador sets it on a room, is still gold into
Haafingar's box, because there is one treasury per hold and the
embassy is in that hold. See [Gold](gold.md).

Carriage to Solitude is Solitude's carriage, 20 gold, not an embassy
fare. See [Carriages and ferries](carriages.md).

## Who may play it

Imperial citizens, merchants, embassy staff, the Ambassador's people.
A Legion soldier under arms is one of the 12, inside the walls. Walk
out the gate carrying and you have broken the Accord.

You are watched, and you are one bad night from being told to leave.
That is the Jarl's scene and the Ambassador's. It is not a profession.

## Where this is

The Accord is on the site. The building is in the world as a place
that can be claimed like any other door, and nothing yet reserves it
for an office, caps a roll at 40, or counts the 12. Solitude's box
and board already exist and are the hold's.
