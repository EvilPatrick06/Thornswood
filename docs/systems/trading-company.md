# Free Skyrim Trading Company

Skyrim's hauler, and a business. A haul is a job on a notice board
with a pay and a destination hold. The company is the tenth that
stays in the box when the haul is confirmed. It also makes, sells,
and buys.

It is not the East Empire Company. The Accord is grain, wine, paper
and worked goods coming in, and ore and timber going out, and the
company is how a crate gets from one hold to another for pay. The
lore of that agreement is on the site. This page is the coin.

Carriages and ferries move people. They are not the company. See
[Carriages and ferries](carriages.md). A lot of goods at once go on a cart that
somebody drives; see [Trade carts](carts.md).

## Posting a haul

At the board of the hold you are sending from, click the board and
fill in Send a haul: the pay, the town it goes to, and what it
carries. The town is one of the ten, and not this one. What it
carries is what the job says.

Decided by Patrick on 4 Oct: nothing a person does in the world is a
chat command. A haul is the board's buttons, the same as a notice
or a job.

The server takes two sums before the job goes up:

- the job fee, 30 gold
- the pay, in full, which is never halved

Both go onto this hold's ledger. If the pack cannot cover both,
nothing is taken and nothing is posted.

The job shows on the board as a job, with the pay and the destination
in the line.

## Taking it

Take, on that board, the same as any job. One taker. Hand it back,
on the board, hands it back, and the haul comes down.

On a hand back, or on the poster's Take down before anybody has
taken it, the pay returns to the poster's pack and the ledger goes down by that
pay. The job fee stays. The company's tenth is not taken, because
nothing was delivered.

A steward at the posting board can take down an open or a taken haul
the same way: the pay returns, the fee stays. There is no clock. A haul
sits until a person confirms it or cancels it. The server does not
watch the road, and it does not look in the hauler's pack for the
crate.

## Confirming it

At the destination hold's board, the poster or a steward presses
Confirm on the haul, under Hauls that came here. A haul shows there
only at the board of the town it named, once somebody has taken it.
At the posting board there is nothing to confirm. The person who took
it does not confirm their own haul.

A steward here is a steward of that board's hold: whoever holds its
"Steward of <Hold>" Discord role, or its Jarl (#2551). The Steward of
the destination confirms a haul at its board, and the Steward of the
posting hold takes one down at its board. Decided by Patrick on 4 Oct
(#1646): "the person who posted or a steward."

On confirm, the company keeps a tenth of the pay, rounded down to a
whole gold. A pay under 10 gold keeps nothing, and the hauler receives
all of it. The rest is handed to the hauler as gold. The ledger drops
by what the hauler received. The tenth, and the job fee, stay.

| Pay posted | Company keeps | Hauler receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | 0 | 9 |
| 10 | 1 | 9 |
| 25 | 2 | 23 |
| 80 | 8 | 72 |
| 100 | 10 | 90 |

Posting an 80 gold haul costs 30 plus 80, which is 110, onto the
ledger. On delivery the hauler receives 72. The ledger keeps 38:
the 30 fee and the 8 tenth. There is no guild membership discount.

The gold the hauler receives was the poster's. Creating it back into
a pack and subtracting it from the ledger is the box paying out. It
is not a new mint. See [Gold](gold.md).

Somebody who is away when gold is theirs, the hauler on a confirm or
the poster on a hand back or a take down, is handed it the next time
they are in the world. Until then it stays on the ledger, held for
them, and nothing else can spend it. A confirm is not refused because
the hauler is away. Decided by Patrick on 4 Oct (#1646).

A thing delivered to somebody who is not there to take it, a crate from
another hold among them, is left in the hold's drop box on the notice
board, with their name on it, and that hold's steward hands it out
(Patrick, 12 Oct, #2298). See [Notice boards](noticeboards.md), the drop
box.

## Who runs it

The Free Skyrim Trading Company is a faction. Its lead seat is one of
the Faction Leader applications in Discord, the same shape as a Jarl.
A faction has one leader.

The people who are accepted run it as a business: they make, they
sell, and they buy. What they wear on the road while a crate is in
the pack is their own affair, in the world. It is not a server rule.

Nobody is handed a company ware before the first haul has moved.
The Riekling pieces that stay in the open book are company wares as
well as race locked. They are named on [Cut items](cut-items.md).

## Sales that leave the world

Sometimes there is an event where someone sells a thing out of
Skyrim. The thing leaves the game. That is the point: it is gone, and
it does not come back into a chest.

It is a trade. The worker and the seller open an ordinary trade, with
the key on the prompt that appears when you look at somebody, and the
worker presses Buy for the company on the trade panel. Only somebody
the company has taken on sees that button. The seller puts up the
thing. The worker puts up the price as gold. An item has no gold
price of its own: the price is the one the two of them agree to on
the panel, and both see the split before they agree.

When the company buys, the worker who handled the sale takes 10% of
the price, rounded down to a whole gold. Another 10% leaves the
server. The seller receives what is left. A price under 10 gold pays
the seller the whole price, and neither cut is taken. See
[Gold](gold.md).

The price is the worker's own gold, from their pack. No hold's ledger
pays for a sale or moves with one. Decided by Patrick on 4 Oct
(#1646): "Most of these can only be got from the trading company, so
the hold's ledger makes no sense."

## What is not a haul

A sale across a counter in the same town is a trade. Rent is rent. A
notice is a notice. A carriage fare is a fare. None of those pay the
tenth, and none of them are sent with Send a haul.

## Where this is

Hauls are in the server, on the board's panel: Send a haul takes the
30 fee and the pay, and both go onto that hold's ledger. The wall
shows a haul with its pay and its town. Take, Hand it back and Take
down are the board's buttons, the same as for any job, and a steward
sees Take down on every haul at that board. Confirm, under Hauls that
came here at the destination's board, pays the hauler out of the
ledger and keeps the tenth. A confirm answers only a haul, never an
ordinary job, and the server still pays nothing for an ordinary job.
Gold for somebody who is away waits on the ledger, held for them.

The ledger goes down only for these payouts and a letter's 15. A
withdrawal never leaves less on the ledger than the open letters and
hauls still hold, and what a haul holds for somebody away.

The lead of the company is a Faction Leader ticket in Discord, Apply:
Faction Leader, picking Free Skyrim Trading Company. The role that
lets a character handle a company sale is the company's member role,
Free Skyrim Trading Company (it was called Trading Company until #3317),
given and taken by the company's Leader on the faction panel in
#free-skyrim-trading-company-seats, in the company's own category.
`bot/Build-Discord.py` makes that role, and `access.company` in the
site's config names it. A sale out of Skyrim is the trade panel's Buy
for the company, as above. The things the seller put up leave the
seller's pack and the world. The worker pays the price less their
10%, another 10% is destroyed, and the seller receives the rest.
Under 10 gold the seller receives the whole price. No ledger is
touched by a sale: the price comes from the worker's pack, as decided
on #1646. A steward is the Steward or the Jarl of the board's hold,
by the Discord role (`isStewardOf` in the property block), at that
hold's board alone.
