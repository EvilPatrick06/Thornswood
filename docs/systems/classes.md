# Classes

Where the raw material comes from. One class. It has nothing to do
with the profession. A Miner who is a Smith digs their own ore. A
Smith who is a Hunter does not, and has to know somebody who did.

Until a class is picked, every seam, carcass, plant, fishing spot and
crop turns you away.

The hours are the profession ladder, and the count starts again at
each rank: 100 pieces to advanced, 250 to expert, 600 to master.
One successful use of the verb is one piece. See
[Professions](professions.md) for the rest cost and the party bonus,
which apply here too.

A rank never stops you carrying or eating the thing. It decides what
you may take out of the world.

## Tools

The miner, the farmer, the woodworker, and the fisher need a tool in
the pack. It does not have to be equipped. It has 50 uses. You are
told when it is worn thin. When it breaks, the next one is made.
Nothing hands you a free replacement.

| Who | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| Miner | Any pickaxe except a war pick. The ancient Nordic pick and the Notched Pickaxe both count. A smith makes the next one |
| Farmer | A shovel. Skyrim has no hoe. Two shovels that look alike both count. A smith makes the next one |
| Woodworker | A woodcutter's axe. This is the profession, not a class. It is named here because it is the same 50 uses |
| Fisher | A fishing pole. Crude for novice water, Fine through expert, Master for all of it. A woodwright makes the next one |

Gathering and hunting need your hands.

You are handed the tool at the moment the pick is made, and a farmer
is also handed seed. See [Creation](creation.md).

## Where this is

The five verbs, the pick, the shovel, the axe, the rank gates and
the lists are in the server. The fishing verb refuses without a
pole, and the new water is not placed. The numbers for how long a
seam, a plant, a fish and a crop take are on each class page. They
are the plan's numbers. Every tool in the server has 50 uses: the
pick, the shovel, the axe, the pole, and the one handed at the pick.
