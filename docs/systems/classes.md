# Classes

The half of the world that produces rather than makes. One class. It
has nothing to do with the profession. A Miner who is a Smith digs
their own ore. A Smith who is a Hunter does not.

Until a class is picked, none of the five verbs works.

The hours to the next rank are the profession ladder: 100, 250, 600.
A rank never stops you carrying or eating the thing. It decides what
you may take out of the world.

## Hunter

Only a hunter loots an animal body. Everyone else walks past it.

112 kinds of body, 361 placed records:
[lists/hunter.md](lists/hunter.md).

A body's rank is the gold of its best part: up to 10 novice, up to 25
advanced, up to 75 expert, and master past that. The yield on the list
is the least and the most of each part, novice first. A pair of zeroes
means that rank may not work that body. A novice rabbit is 1 to 2 of
what it gives. A master on the same body is 1 to 5.

The body stays down long enough to be worked. People, bandits, draugr
and dragons are not this list.

## Fisher

Press E at the water. No rod, and the rods from the fishing mod do
nothing.

[lists/fisher.md](lists/fisher.md). Salmon, clams, pearl oysters,
horker meat and slaughterfish egg nests are the verb. Six names, fifteen
placed records.

## Gatherer

Reagents out of the world. Food plants are the farmer's.

46 names, 105 placed records:
[lists/gatherer.md](lists/gatherer.md).

Ranked by what the reagent sells for: 1 gold novice, up to 5 advanced,
up to 12 expert, master above that.

## Miner

A pickaxe in the pack, 100 uses. Any pickaxe except a war pick. When it
breaks, the next one comes from a smith.

18 ores, 581 veins: [lists/miner.md](lists/miner.md).

Ranked by the ore's price: up to 14 gold novice, up to 29 advanced, up
to 59 expert, master above that. Heart stone is advanced even though
its price would say master, so a Bladebinder's staves line up with the
stone. A vein gives a least and a most per swing. Zeroes mean that rank
cannot work it. A seam holds about twenty and comes back in ten or
thirty minutes.

## Farmer

A shovel in the pack, 100 uses. Skyrim has no hoe. Seed is handed once,
when the class is picked. After that, seed is a crop you kept.

10 crops, 14 records: [lists/farmer.md](lists/farmer.md).

| Rank | Crops |
| --- | --- |
| Novice | Cabbage, leek, potato, gourd, carrot |
| Advanced | Wheat, ash yam, tomato |
| Expert | The orchard |
| Master | Everything on the list |

Ranked by how hard it is to grow, not by the price. A farm is claimed,
one at a time. The first harvest of a wild row is yours. After that
the row is empty until it is sown. How long a crop takes, how much it
gives, and how long an unworked claim lasts are server numbers and
move during the alpha.

## Eggs and what is not on a ladder

Eggs and a little wild food are listed apart from a carcass:

Plants the harvest table does not rank:
[lists/unranked.md](lists/unranked.md). A chicken nest is the farmer's

## Where this is

The five verbs, the tools, the ranks on each table, and the lists are
in the server. The pages linked above are those tables, one name per
row, with how many placed records sit behind the name.

Crop timers and the unworked-claim timer are set on the server and are
still expected to move. They are not copied here as a finished clock.
