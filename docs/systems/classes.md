# Classes

Where the raw material comes from. One class. It has nothing to do
with the profession. A Miner who is a Smith digs their own ore. A
Smith who is a Hunter does not, and has to know somebody who did.

Until a class is picked, every seam, carcass, plant, fishing spot and
crop turns you away.

The hours are the profession ladder, and the count starts again at
each rank: 100 pieces to advanced, 250 to expert, 600 to master.
One successful use of the verb is one piece. See
[Professions](professions.md) for the rest cost and the party bonus,
which apply here too.

A rank never stops you carrying or eating the thing. It decides what
you may take out of the world.

## Tools

Three picks need a tool in the pack. It does not have to be equipped.
It has 50 uses. You are told when it is worn thin. When it breaks,
the next one comes from a smith. Nothing hands you a free replacement.

| Who | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| Miner | Any pickaxe except a war pick. The ancient Nordic pick and the Notched Pickaxe both count |
| Farmer | A shovel. Skyrim has no hoe. Two shovels that look alike both count |
| Woodworker | A woodcutter's axe. This is the profession, not a class. It is named here because it is the same 50 uses |

Gathering, hunting and fishing need your hands.

You are handed the tool at the moment the pick is made, and a farmer
is also handed seed. See [Creation](creation.md).
