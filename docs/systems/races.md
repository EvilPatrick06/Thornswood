A race is the face and the gate on a people's own things. It is not a
class and not a profession. Every race can be any class and any
profession.

## What locks

A recipe locks to a race when the thing is that people's dress, or when
the thing is riekling gear.

Locked sets, and the race that may make them:

## What a riekling wears

Clothes made for the other peoples have no riekling cut: on a riekling
they are drawn at the size of a person, with the neck in the wrong place.
Only the riekling pieces from the riekling plugin fit. A new riekling
starts in a riekling loincloth and cloth hand and foot wraps, and a
riekling Haberdasher makes those, the second loincloth and the fur cloak.
The riekling smith makes the fur and chief armour. Decided by Patrick on
12 October 2026.

## What does not lock

A material is a smithing tier. Any smith of the right half and rank makes
it. Elven, Orcish, Nordic Carved, Ancient Nord and Nord Hero stay open.
Imperial armour that is legion issue stays open. Colovian is a province's
dress and does lock. Nord Mail locks. Nordic does not.

Thalmor robes are a faction, not a people. Forsworn gear is a tribe's kit,
not a race. Einherjar, Barbarian, Warchief, Stormlord and Viking are
flavour names on armour. None of those lock.

Falmer, Dremora, Draugr and the other races nobody can choose do not lock
anything. A lock on a race that cannot be picked would delete the recipe.

## Where this is

The twelve races, the word list, and the race field on each recipe are in
the tables, and the craft gate reads that field. The list page is the
whole of what is locked today. A new cultural set is a new row in the
word list, then a rebuild of the recipe table, or it will stay open.
