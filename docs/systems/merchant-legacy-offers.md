Already accepted original work remains visible and recoverable through its
former compatible trade. Kill turn-ins still require their original hold.
New acceptance, including a direct service call, requires the appointed
reference. Abandoning or completing the old work does not unlock another copy
at a different specialist. Saved original records and opaque fields are
preserved; no migration or reset occurs.

Final acceptance checks the offering merchant before reading the final
progression snapshot. Only the captured session check follows that read.
Regressions reproduce duplicate offers and a late callback changing standing,
and confirm zero writes on refusal, original compatible recovery and the ten
exact roster/role bindings.

The six other scarce resources and seven retrievals remain unbound in this
section. Their interaction, source and dedicated-item work must be completed
separately before activation. The241 parcel commissions and four expanded
combat offers retain their immutable appointed references and terms.
