# People beside you

Friends, a party, a mute, a trade, a journal, a bard, and the wheel.
None of these is a class or a profession.

## Friends

Look at somebody and press Y, or `/friend`. They have to accept. Both
sides are written. A list with one name in it is not a friendship.

`/friend` with nothing after it lists them, and says who is away.
`/friend remove` and the name takes one off. P opens the same list.

There is no cap written down. The plan keeps it that way until a list
is long enough to be a problem. A friend is not a party and does not
grant the work bonus.

## A party

Look at them and offer, or `/party`. They accept. `/party` lists who
is in it. `/party leave` drops you. P opens it.

The work bonus is 15% of a piece, banked, paid as one extra piece when
the bank reaches a whole one. That is every seventh piece of work.
It pays when somebody from the party is in the same cell. Being on
the list and standing in another hold pays nothing.

The bonus applies to every trade that already goes through the work
counter: a seam, a log, a fish, a craft, a kill that counts.

There is no party cap in the server. The plan is 6, including you.
A seventh accept is refused. Six is enough for a scene and small
enough that the 15% does not become a second ladder.

## Mute and voice

Look at them and press L, or `/mute`. You stop hearing them. It does
not stop them hearing you. It is not a staff silence.

Voice is the launcher, not the game. Hold V to talk. O leaves the
microphone open. B steps the distance:

| Voice | About |
| --- | --- |
| Whisper | 7 metres |
| Ordinary | 30 metres |
| Yell | 85 metres |

The launcher has to be open. Muffle and Invisibility do not change
these distances.

## Trade

Look at them and press H, or `/trade`. Both put up goods and a gold
amount. Both confirm. You cannot put up more gold than you are
holding. Gold does not buy a rank. See [Gold](gold.md).

A trade is not a haul and it is not the Merchant's Guild. Those are
[Free Skyrim Trading Company](trading-company.md) and
[Merchant's Guild](merchants-guild.md).

## Journal

J opens it. `/journal` is the same. It is your pages, on the
character, not a book in the world. Another person does not read it
unless you show them. It is not a quest log. Vanilla quests do not
run.

## Bard

`/bard` in a cell. For 5 minutes, anyone resting in that cell recovers
rest faster: +25 on top of the place, if the bard is still the one
who said it and the 5 minutes have not gone. The places themselves
are on [Exhaustion](exhaustion.md). Sit 20, campfire 35, inn 50, bed
80, per real hour, and the bard adds 25 to whichever of those is
running.

Bard is not a profession. The lute, flute, and drum on the emote wheel
are animations. They do not count as `/bard` unless you also say the
word. Instruments as items are Woodwright recipes when the table says
so.

## The wheel

G opens it. Six categories, 34 emotes. Tab or the middle walks the
categories. N stands you up. The wheel does not open in the creation
hall.

Greeting: Wave, Salute, Bow, At ease, Come here.

Praise: Applaud, Ovation, Slow clap, Cheer, Laugh.

Gesture: Point, Point yonder, Examine, Lean, Surrender, Nervous.

Devotion: Pray, Kneel, Blessing.

Labour: Warm hands, Warm arms, Sweep, Clean blade, Chop wood, Write,
Read, Drink, Eat.

Revelry: Dance, Jig, Reel, Lute, Flute, Drum.

Chop wood, Drink, and Eat on the wheel do not perform the class verb,
the `/drink`, or the `/eat`. Those are the words and the presses.
The wheel is the animation.

## Out of character

`/ooc` or `/o`, and the words. Heard by whoever is near. It is how a
scene asks for the yes on [Death](death.md), and how anybody in it
ends it. `/ooc can we not` ends the scene. It does not undo what
already happened.

## Where this is

Friends, party, mute, trade, journal, `/bard` for 5 minutes, the
wheel, and `/ooc` are in the server. The party bonus of 15% is in the
server. A cap of 6 is not. The emote Chop wood is not a chop.
