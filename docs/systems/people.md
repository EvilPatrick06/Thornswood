A friend is not a party. A party is who is in the cell with you this
evening, and it is what pays the work bonus. The person who opened
it leads it. A trade is two people and what they are holding. A
friend request, a party invite, and a trade offer are answered on a
prompt, with Accept and Refuse, and the offer stands for 45 seconds.
A journal is yours until you share a page with a friend, including
one who is not online. They see that page while you are online. A faction
or a business is a second tab on that same panel, and most people
never see it. Voice is how far a spoken word carries, and a colored
mark above the head while the microphone is open. The wheel is
animations: eight quick slots in the middle, and every emote in a
searchable list. Playing one puts its name on the same line as typed
speech. The number keys 1 to 8 play a slot with the wheel shut. Text
is what you type. A plain line, a `/me`, a `/do`, a whisper, a shout,
and an `/ooc` also sit above the head for whoever is close enough to
read them. Out of character stays in brackets, including there, so a
scene can see that it stepped out. You can stop hearing one person,
stop reading them, or hide their name, and only for yourself, from
P, for a friend or anybody near you. Hide every name, on P, hides
every name. A staff silence is everybody's,
and the person cannot lift it.

## Bard

Play the lute, the flute or the drum on the [emote wheel](emotes.md),
with that instrument in your pack, or Sing on the wheel, which needs
nothing. While you play, and for 5 minutes after, anyone resting in
that cell recovers rest faster: +25 on top of the place, while you
are still in the cell. The places themselves are on
[Exhaustion](exhaustion.md). A seat 20, a seat by a fire 35, an inn or
temple bed nobody holds 40, a bed you may use 50, your own bed 80, per
real hour, and the bard adds 25
to whichever of those is running. Patrick, 4 Oct: "instrument emotes
require the instrument, so if they do an emote with an instrument
have that count as /bard, and also add a singing emote for people
that are only singing".

Bard is not a profession. Nothing is typed. The server sees the
playing: the last animation the game sent for you, and the
instrument in your pack.

## Where this is

The systems in the table are in the server, with the gaps named at
the bottom of each page. A faction or a business is the group book
on J. The bard, from the wheel's lute, flute, drum and Sing, is in the
server.
The party cap of 6 is in the server. A friend request, a party
invite, and a trade offer are answered on the prompt. The wheel's
eight quick slots, the search, and the sixty emotes are in the
server. Plain speech in the box is local on the dev voice service,
same cell, about 57 metres. Left unset, a plain line still goes to
everybody, which is how the live voice service is left. Text mute,
hiding a name, hiding every name, and the leader's kick, lead, and
disband are in the server. Staff
silence is a staff command. It stops their voice, what they type, the
words above the head, and the speaking mark. It does not hide a name.
The mark, the words, and an emote's name are drawn above the head.
