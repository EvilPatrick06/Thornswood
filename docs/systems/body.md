# The body

Optional, and later. It is not part of going live. After the server
is fully live, it is added only if a vote says yes. Until that vote,
nobody builds it.

A slow meter, 0 to 100. It starts at 0. It fills as you eat and drink.
It is not hunger and it is not thirst.

## What fills it

| Thing | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Clean water, a potion sip | 2 |
| Bread, a dry ration, fruit | 5 |
| A cooked meal | 12 |
| Raw meat | 14 |
| Ale, each one | 18 |
| A feast | 20 |

At 60 you are uncomfortable: -2 speed, and rest recovery is 10% slower.
At 85 a merchant in town can refuse the trade until you have dealt with
it. The refusal is the server answering the trade. Guards do not
comment. In the wild there is nobody to refuse you. You are still
uncomfortable.

At 100 it does not get worse than 85. The meter stops at 100.

## Relief

An outhouse or a pot in a home you may use sets it to 0 and gives +10
rest recovery for 10 minutes. Stepping off the road sets it to 0 and
gives nothing else. Either one takes 15 seconds, and you cannot do it
in front of a fight.

It should matter across a long ruin or a long evening. It should not
ask for a trip every few minutes. If it does, the points above are too
high.

## Where this is

Not in the server, and not work to do before the vote. Hunger, thirst
and rest do not stand in for it. Outhouses and pots are not furniture
the server knows. Leave them that way until a vote after the server
is fully live says to add this meter.
