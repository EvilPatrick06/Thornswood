# Who makes what the world hands out

Generated by `scripts/dev/Check-Makers.js` from the server's own tables, as `game/data/item-sources.json` reads them. Do not edit it by hand: run `node scripts/dev/Check-Makers.js --write`.

Everything in the world has a recipe except named uniques and quest items (Patrick, 22 October 2026, #3132). The world here is what the server puts into a pack from nothing: the dungeon and body loot ([Dungeon loot](../dungeon-loot.md)), a new character's clothes, the tool a class pick hands over, a farmer's first seeds, and whatever a recipe in the open book, a hair dye or a bottle of water asks for. A trader sells only what somebody sold or hauled to it, so traders are not on this page.

What the world hands out and who makes it both come from [Where every item comes from](item-sources.md) (`game/data/item-sources.json`). A maker is a recipe in the open book (not cut and not tempering), a class that gathers the item, or the scarce work or rare seam a class works. A bottle the alchemy lab brews is a new form, not a record, so the lab is the maker of no record. The build fails when the world hands out something with no maker that `scripts/dev/Check-Makers.waiting.json` does not name with the issue that will give it one.

283 items handed out. 164 have a maker. 119 have none yet.
