# Emotes

The wheel of animations. G opens it. It does not open in the creation
hall. You are picking a face, not waving.

Tab, or the middle button, walks the categories. N stands you up.
Escape closes it.

Six categories, 34 emotes.

| Category | Emotes |
| --- | --- |
| Greeting | Wave, Salute, Bow, At ease, Come here |
| Praise | Applaud, Ovation, Slow clap, Cheer, Laugh |
| Gesture | Point, Point yonder, Examine, Lean, Surrender, Nervous |
| Devotion | Pray, Kneel, Blessing |
| Labour | Warm hands, Warm arms, Sweep, Clean blade, Chop wood, Write, Read, Drink, Eat |
| Revelry | Dance, Jig, Reel, Lute, Flute, Drum |

Chop wood, Drink, and Eat on the wheel do not perform the woodcutter's
verb, `/drink`, or `/eat`. Those are the words and the presses. The
wheel is the animation.

The lute, the flute, and the drum do not count as `/bard`. Bardic
rest is the word, on [People beside you](people.md). `/bard` does
not float above the head. A line that should be seen does, and it
is typed.

## Above the head

Playing an emote puts its name above your head, in the same one line
as typed speech. The reach, the 6 seconds, and the 80 characters are
on [Text chat](chat.md). A wave reads as the action, the way a `/me`
does. It does not also print a line in the box. A newer typed line
replaces it, and another emote replaces a typed line.

Chop wood, Drink, and Eat still do not perform the class verbs. The
name above the head does not make them a chop, a drink, or a meal.

## Where this is

The wheel, the six categories, and the 34 emotes are in the server.
Chop wood on the wheel is not a chop. Nothing from the wheel is
drawn above a head.
