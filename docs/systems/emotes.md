# Emotes

The wheel of animations. G opens it and G closes it. It does not open
in the creation hall. You are picking a face, not waving.

The middle of the wheel is eight quick slots. Click a slot to play
it. The same eight are the number keys 1 to 8, and those play with
the wheel shut, so the wheel does not have to come up at all. To put
an emote on a slot, open the wheel, click that slot's number beside
the list, then click the name. The eight are remembered on the
machine. A fresh install starts as Wave, Bow, Laugh, Point, Pray,
Dance, Lute, Drink.

On the right is every emote, in alphabetical order, and a search.
Tab moves to the search. Click a name to play it. There are no
categories.

N stands you up. The middle of the wheel, marked STOP, stands you
up and closes the wheel. Escape closes it without playing anything.

Fifty nine emotes.

Ache, Agitated, Applaud, Arms crossed, At ease, Attention, Bar drink,
Blessing, Bow, Cheer, Chop wood, Clean blade, Come here, Crouch warm,
Dance, Drink, Drink potion, Drum, Eat, Examine, Flute, Get attention,
Give, Grave bow, Hard clap, Hoe, Horn, Jig, Kneel, Laugh, Lean,
Ledger, Long clap, Look far, Lute, Nervous, Oath, Ovation, Pick up,
Point, Point farther, Point yonder, Pray, Read, Read book, Reel,
Salute, Sit, Slow clap, Study, Surrender, Sweep, Take, Warm arms,
Warm close, Warm hands, Wave, Wipe brow, Write.

Chop wood, Drink, Eat, Drink potion, and Hoe on the wheel do not
perform the woodcutter's verb, `/drink`, `/eat`, or a potion. Those
are the words and the presses. The wheel is the animation.

The lute, the flute, and the drum do not count as `/bard`. Bardic
rest is the word, on [People beside you](people.md). `/bard` does
not float above the head. A line that should be seen does, and it
is typed.

## Above the head

Playing an emote puts its name above your head, in the same one line
as typed speech. The reach, the 6 seconds, and the 80 characters are
on [Text chat](chat.md). A wave reads as the action, the way a `/me`
does. It does not also print a line in the box. A newer typed line
replaces it, and another emote replaces a typed line.

Chop wood, Drink, Eat, Drink potion, and Hoe still do not perform
the class verbs. The name above the head does not make them a chop,
a drink, or a meal.

## Where this is

The wheel, the fifty nine emotes, the eight quick slots, the search,
the alphabetical list, and the number keys are in the server. The
slots are remembered on the machine. Chop wood on the wheel is not a
chop. Nothing from the wheel is drawn above a head.
