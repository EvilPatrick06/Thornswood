# Masters and apprentices

How someone learns a trade that is not the one they picked, and how
the other half of their own profession climbs past novice. This is
its own system. The ladder, the benches, and the recipe lists stay on
[Professions](professions.md). Casting stays on
[Magic and the College](magic.md).

A master is a person at the master rank of one half, or of one school
if they are a mage. The master perk is that rank's name: Warlord,
Archmage, Master Chemist, Grand Chef, Mastercraft, Twin Souls, Master
Weaver, Master Carver. The perk is not a title. Archmage of the
College, and a Jarl, are titles given in the world. See
[Offices](offices.md). A title does not teach anyone.

There are two arrangements. They are not the same slot.

## The sister half

When you pick a half, the other half of that profession is yours at
novice. It costs nothing. It does not use one of the two outside
trades.

You can make that half's novice list. An Edgewright can beat out
novice plate. A steel cuirass is not novice plate.

Work on the sister half still counts, on that half, one piece at a
time, on the same ladder as your own: 100 to advanced, 250 to expert,
600 to master. The count starts again at each rank. Until a master of
that half has taken you on, the count stops at the next threshold and
waits. The next piece after they take you on is the one that raises
you. After that, the sister half climbs the whole ladder. It is still
not your profession's other name. You remain the half you picked.

Taking you on is the master's decision. Nobody is owed it. There is
no fee, and there is no distance written down. It is an agreement the
master makes, and the server records it. Either of you can end it: the
master, or you (Patrick, 4 October). Ranks you already earned on that
half stay. Further ranks stop, and the count waits at the next
threshold again.

The sister half does not hand you that half's placed skill point.
You get the placed point of the half you picked, and no second line
from owning the other one at novice. A rank you do earn on it, once
a master has taken you on, hands the same skill-tree point any rank
hands. See [Skill tree](skill-tree.md).

Inside one profession this is the only way past novice on the half
you did not pick. A master of your own profession cannot use the
outside-trade rules on you. Those rules refuse, and say so.

## An outside trade

A master can teach the novice list of their own half to someone whose
profession is not that one.

All of these have to be true:

1. You already picked your own profession. There is nothing to teach
   onto a character who has not.
2. The teacher is master in that exact half. A master Edgewright
   cannot teach plate.
3. You do not already know that half.
4. You have fewer than two outside trades. The sister half does not
   count toward the two.
5. It is not a half of your own profession. That door is the sister
   half, above.

What you receive is the novice list. The book on K gains a column for
that half, kinds and counts, the same as your own. You can make those
novice recipes. You are refused anything above novice, and the line
says the ceiling is the teaching, not a rank you have failed to grind.

The count of work on a taught half is kept, and it sits at the novice
ceiling. It never raises the rank, however long you spend on it.

An outside trade does not change your profession. It does not hand
you that profession's placed points, and it does not hand you that
half's placed point. It does not give you the class that feeds the
trade. A smith taught to cook is not a farmer. A tailor taught a
school is not a mage.

Two in a life. Either of you can end one. Ending it takes the novice
list away. A taught school or a taught Bladebinder keeps the spells
already learned from it, and no new one can be learned (Patrick,
4 October). It does not open a third.

## A mage teaching someone who is not a mage

A person who is not a mage casts nothing until a master of one school
takes them on. That school is one of the two outside trades. It is
one school, not a second and a third.

They hold 2 spells at most, novice, learned from the tome. The three
gates on [Magic and the College](magic.md) still apply: a semester's
point and a place under those 2, the spell is novice, and a novice
spell needs nothing below it. They do not earn the mage's stored spell
points. They do not pick schools on K. They do not receive the
school's placed point.

At the College, Study on the K panel hands them 1 spell point in a
semester, and it can only be spent on a novice spell of the school they
were taught.
A mage's semester is a different number, on that page.

The third school a master mage picks for themselves is not this. That
is their own profession, at master, and it is not one of anybody
else's two trades.

## A staff, taught

A person who is not an enchanter learns a staff spell only from its
tome, after a master Bladebinder teaches them Bladebinder. That
teaching is one of the two outside trades. Novice staves only, two at
most. Those two places are not the mage's two spells. A person who
was taught a school and was also taught Bladebinder can hold 2 novice
spells and 2 novice staff spells. Both teachings spend the two outside
trades, so there is no third.

A Wardweaver already holds Bladebinder as the sister half. Raising
that past novice is the sister-half arrangement, not an outside trade.
See [Enchanter](professions/enchanter.md).

## What a master is for, by profession

| Master of | What the teaching is |
| --- | --- |
| Bulwark or Berserker | Novice combat for someone who is not a warrior: that half's novice maneuver, Set or Rush. There is no recipe. |
| One school | Novice spells of that school, for someone who is not a mage. Two spells, from the tome. |
| Blightbrewer or Physicker | Novice brewing of that half. The lab has no recipe rows, so novice is the bench, not a list. |
| Hearthcook or Confectioner | The novice meals of that half. |
| Platewright or Edgewright | The novice pieces of that half. |
| Bladebinder | Novice staves, and two staff spells from the tome if the student is not an enchanter. |
| Wardweaver | Novice enchanting of armour and clothing. No recipe rows. |
| Haberdasher or Liverywright | The novice garments of that half. |
| Charburner or Woodwright | The novice kiln goods, or the novice woodwork, of that half. |

Repair kits follow the same halves. A Platewright's kit, an
Edgewright's kit, and a Haberdasher's kit are the three repairs on
[Durability](durability.md). The 120% repair is the half the person
picked doing the work, at Master for the two smith kits (#3442). The
sister half never gives it, at any rank,
even once a master has taken that half past novice, and neither does
an outside trade (Patrick, 4 October). Everybody else repairs to
100%.

## What teaching does not do

It does not sell a rank. Gold does not buy one. See [Gold](gold.md).

It does not let a taught trade feed a mage's schools. A school you
were taught never leaves novice, so work is not counted toward a
ladder you cannot climb. A mage's own schools are a different rule,
on the mage page: a rank they earn somewhere else is 10 work in every
school they hold.

It does not put you in a party, and it does not change the party
bonus. Work done while somebody from your party is in the same cell
still banks 15% of a piece, including work on a taught half.

## Where this is

Picking a profession writes the sister half at novice. The craft gate
allows that novice list, and it allows a taught half at novice, and
it refuses both of them above novice by name.

Nothing here is typed. A master looking at somebody they could take
on sees a row for it on the look prompt, Take on as apprentice, named
K. K then opens the Masters and apprentices page of the K panel on that
person, with a Take on button for each half or school it could be, so
a master of two picks which there. The person accepts or refuses on
the prompt, the same as a party or a trade, and nothing is recorded
until they accept. The server reads which arrangement it is off the
person: the other half of their own profession is the sister half, and
a half or school of another profession is an outside trade.

The same page lists who you took on and who took you on, each with
End, and how many of your two outside trades are used. Either of you
ends it there, and End asks twice. The buttons send the server a line,
and no help text names it.

Both characters carry the arrangement, so it holds across a restart
and with either of them offline. When work is counted, the master is
passed in while they are still a master of that half and still list
the person, so the sister half rises past the threshold it waited at.
An outside trade that was ended stays counted, so it does not open a
third.

The two-spell cap, the two staff-spell cap, and the three gates on a
tome are in the server. So is the semester: Study on the K panel's
College page, in one of the College's rooms, hands a person a master
has taught 1 point once in 24 hours. The server asks for that point as
well as a place before a tome teaches them, because the semester is the
only thing that hands them one. Patrick decided on 4 October that they
need both (issue 1387). See [Magic and the College](magic.md).
