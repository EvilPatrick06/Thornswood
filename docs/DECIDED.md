# Decided

What Patrick decided against, and the settled rules of the game, in one
place. Read it before filing a new idea or feature (Developer-Guide.md, the
board). Each line links where the decision was made. An idea that goes
against one of these says so in its issue and asks for the decision to be
changed; it is not filed as if the question were open.

A decision that changes moves here the day it changes, with the new date and
link, and the old line goes.

## The world is run by people

- **Guards are people, never NPCs.** The Jarl of each hold names that hold's
  guards. 12 Oct 2026,
  [#502](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/502#issuecomment-6132845221).
- **No bounty system.** Nothing gives a bounty: killing, attacking, theft,
  pickpocketing or trespass. 12 Oct 2026,
  [#502](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/502#issuecomment-6132845221).
  A bounty or wanted poster that people write and post themselves is
  allowed; only Skyrim's own bounty system is off. 21 Oct 2026,
  [#3174](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3174#issuecomment-6333339753).
- **Hold laws and smuggling are roleplay only.** A Jarl posts the hold's law
  on the notice board and guards enforce it; nothing in the game checks goods
  or runs smuggling. 21 Oct 2026,
  [#3172](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3172#issuecomment-6333339441),
  [#3173](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3173#issuecomment-6333339604).
- **A system never replaces roleplay.** Where people can do a thing between
  themselves (a law, a search, a trade, a sentence), the game gives them the
  place and the record, not an NPC or a timer that does it for them. The two
  lines above are this rule applied.
- **Ship cargo can be taken by anyone at a dock**, and the manifest records
  who took it; whether it was a robbery is roleplay. Cargo nobody collects
  goes to the port's hold drop box after 7 real days (a staff setting).
  22 Oct 2026, [#511](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/511#issuecomment-6334348209).

## Items and coin

- **An item has no gold price.** What it sells for is the price a person
  posts. Nothing is balanced against an item's gold worth. docs/SYSTEMS.md
  (Work) and docs/systems/professions.md; re-cost against gold dropped
  21 Oct 2026,
  [#613](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/613#issuecomment-6326847193).
- **Forge recipes take no gold coins** as an input, only materials. 21 Oct
  2026, Patrick's answer on the report page (B), on
  [#3115](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3115).
- **A bench opens only for the trades that can use it.** 21 Oct 2026,
  [#3120](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3120#issuecomment-6327682356).
- **Jewellery is Smith work** at the forge, either half; a recipe no trade
  owns goes to the trade of the bench it sits at. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3120](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3120#issuecomment-6334348395).
- **Strength is Might.** Oversized weapons need Might 3, the Giant's Club
  and anything heavier Might 5. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#2262](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/2262#issuecomment-6334347980).
- **The vanilla perk system goes entirely**, not only the perk conditions on
  recipes. Jhinswife's work. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#1402](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/1402#issuecomment-6334347763).

## Buttons, not commands

- **Nothing people do in the world is a chat command**; commands are for
  staff and dev. The few that stay commands are listed in
  docs/SYSTEMS.md, "Buttons, not commands". 4 Oct 2026.
- **A problem report has both doors**: the Discord button and an in-game
  button. 21 Oct 2026,
  [#3155](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3155#issuecomment-6333339922).

## The box

- **Builds stay on the game box**, and each build's cost comes down; a
  second box or a public repository is a new question only if the box still
  runs above 60% busy on a day of pull requests. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3188](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3188#issuecomment-6334347216).
- **Windows stays unactivated for now**; no licence is bought until the next
  hosting decision. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3104](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3104#issuecomment-6334347434).
