# Decided

What Patrick decided against, and the settled rules of the game, in one
place. Read it before filing a new idea or feature (Developer-Guide.md, the
board). Each line links where the decision was made. An idea that goes
against one of these says so in its issue and asks for the decision to be
changed; it is not filed as if the question were open.

A decision that changes moves here the day it changes, with the new date and
link, and the old line goes.

## The world is run by people

- **Guards are people, never NPCs.** The Jarl of each hold names that hold's
  guards. 12 Oct 2026,
  [#502](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/502#issuecomment-6132845221).
- **No bounty system.** Nothing gives a bounty: killing, attacking, theft,
  pickpocketing or trespass. 12 Oct 2026,
  [#502](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/502#issuecomment-6132845221).
  A bounty or wanted poster that people write and post themselves is
  allowed; only Skyrim's own bounty system is off. 21 Oct 2026,
  [#3174](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3174#issuecomment-6333339753).
- **Hold laws and smuggling are roleplay only.** A Jarl posts the hold's law
  on the notice board and guards enforce it; nothing in the game checks goods
  or runs smuggling. 21 Oct 2026,
  [#3172](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3172#issuecomment-6333339441),
  [#3173](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3173#issuecomment-6333339604).
- **A system never replaces roleplay.** Where people can do a thing between
  themselves (a law, a search, a trade, a sentence), the game gives them the
  place and the record, not an NPC or a timer that does it for them. The two
  lines above are this rule applied.
- **Ship cargo can be taken by anyone at a dock**, and the manifest records
  who took it; whether it was a robbery is roleplay. Cargo nobody collects
  goes to the port's hold drop box after 7 real days (a staff setting).
  22 Oct 2026, [#511](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/511#issuecomment-6334348209).
- **NPCs nobody can see are updated less often**; the world keeps moving
  with nobody near, at a fraction of the cost. They are never paused. 22 Oct
  2026,
  [#3181](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3181#issuecomment-6335264912).
- **Each person picks which emotes become powers** on the favourites menu,
  if the one-emote prototype works. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#2026](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/2026#issuecomment-6335268809).
- **The cargo register keeps a closed line for 30 days.** 22 Oct 2026,
  [#2858](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/2858#issuecomment-6335269217).
- **Wanted posters are written by people; nothing is automatic.** The game
  detects no crime. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3174](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3174#issuecomment-6335270129).
- **Anyone may put up a wanted poster;** that hold's Jarl and guards can
  take it down. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3174](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3174#issuecomment-6335270129).
- **A poster's reward is put up front** and held until a guard confirms it
  was earned. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3174](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3174#issuecomment-6335270129).
- **Anyone can copy a written book** at a writing desk with paper and ink.
  22 Oct 2026,
  [#3179](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3179#issuecomment-6335270371).
- **A signed contract is read by the people who signed it, and staff.** 22
  Oct 2026,
  [#3177](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3177#issuecomment-6335270611).
- **A blizzard or heavy storm makes travel 15% slower** and adds cold or
  wet; the right gear protects. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3176](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3176#issuecomment-6335270816).
- **Letters and parcels are picked up at any inn in the recipient's hold.**
  22 Oct 2026,
  [#3175](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3175#issuecomment-6335271035).
- **House and shop upkeep is due weekly**, in goods or labour. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3170](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3170#issuecomment-6335271623).
- **Missed upkeep never takes the claim away;** it brings a penalty, the way

  [#3160](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3160#issuecomment-6335273541).
- **People drive the carts** that move goods between holds. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3158](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3158#issuecomment-6335273986).
- **A shipment is lost only when people raid it**, never by chance. 22 Oct

  [#3158](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3158#issuecomment-6335273986).

  [#3144](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3144#issuecomment-6335275250).

  [#1387](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/1387#issuecomment-6335277008).
- **No fine or sentence system;** guards and Jarls roleplay fines and jail

  [#502](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/502#issuecomment-6335277275).
- **Only people the Trading Company has taken on may charter a ship's hold**

  [#512](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/512#issuecomment-6335277509).
- **The company's haul fee and the notice board's job fee are separate.** 22

## Items and coin

- **An item has no gold price.** What it sells for is the price a person
  posts. Nothing is balanced against an item's gold worth. docs/GAME-SYSTEMS.md
  (Work) and docs/systems/professions.md; re-cost against gold dropped
  21 Oct 2026,
  [#613](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/613#issuecomment-6326847193).
- **Forge recipes take no gold coins** as an input, only materials. 21 Oct
  2026, Patrick's answer on the report page (B), on
  [#3115](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3115).
- **A bench opens only for the trades that can use it.** 21 Oct 2026,
  [#3120](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3120#issuecomment-6327682356).
- **Jewellery is Smith work** at the forge, either half; a recipe no trade
  owns goes to the trade of the bench it sits at. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3120](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3120#issuecomment-6334348395).
- **Strength is Might.** Oversized weapons need Might 3, the Giant's Club
  and anything heavier Might 5. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#2262](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/2262#issuecomment-6334347980).
- **The vanilla perk system goes entirely**, not only the perk conditions on
  recipes. Jhinswife's work. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#1402](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/1402#issuecomment-6334347763).
- **Records whose author does not allow us to hand them out are rebuilt as
  our own**, so places keep their look. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#2791](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/2791#issuecomment-6335266988).
- **Every file we ship from Nexus has its author and terms recorded;** any
  with no permission is rebuilt as ours. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#2793](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/2793#issuecomment-6335267215).
  Files made from another mod's records, which cannot be rebuilt, leave for
  good and nobody asks for permission (the four Fishing In Skyrim faces).
  26 Oct 2026,
  [#2793](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/2793#issuecomment-6433239073).
- **Food spoils gently:** raw in 1 real week, cooked in 2, preserved never.
  22 Oct 2026,
  [#3163](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3163#issuecomment-6335272878).
- **A pack does not spoil while its owner is offline;** chests and shops
  keep spoiling. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3163](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3163#issuecomment-6335272878).
- **A drop lies on the ground for 2 minutes, then is gone,** a restart in
  between included, and it keeps what it carried. So food never spoils on
  the ground. 27 Oct 2026,
  [#3498](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3498#issuecomment-6431943093).
- **Hangover: stamina regen down 25% for 10 minutes. Withdrawal after 3 uses
  in a day: stamina and magicka regen down 10% for 30 minutes.** Drunk,
  high, hangover and withdrawal each have visual effects as well (Patrick:
  "I also want visual affects"). 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3153](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3153#issuecomment-6335274417).
- **Seasonal dishes can be made any time** with the ingredients; the
  ingredients are what is seasonal. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3152](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3152#issuecomment-6335274608).
- **Crafted items come in three tiers:** crude, fine and masterwork, at
  minus or plus 10%. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3151](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3151#issuecomment-6335274847).
- **Balance is measured by what a thing takes to make or get, plus its
  stats**, never by gold. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3135](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3135#issuecomment-6335275935).
- **A mixed alchemy recipe is remembered by the character**, at any alchemy
  table. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3134](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3134#issuecomment-6335276146).
- **Everything in the world has a recipe except named uniques and quest
  items.** 22 Oct 2026,
  [#3132](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3132#issuecomment-6335276565).
- **A ship's hold pays a freight fee per load that leaves the world**, as a
  coin sink that helps balance the economy. 22 Oct 2026,
  [#512](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/512#issuecomment-6335277509).

## Voice and the in-game UI

- **Voice leaves the browser:** a native voice engine in the launcher sends
  one stream to the box, and the box forwards each voice only to the people
  in range. Every launcher no longer connects to every other. 26 Oct 2026,
  [#3208](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3208#issuecomment-6422741408).
- **The in-game panels become Skyrim's own menus (Scaleform)**, chat
  included, and the in-game browser goes once they have; the away window is
  built first as the proof. Emotes as Q menu powers stay as #2026 decided.
  26 Oct 2026,
  [#3208](https://github.com/EvilPatrick06/Thornswood/issues/3208#issuecomment-6422741408).
