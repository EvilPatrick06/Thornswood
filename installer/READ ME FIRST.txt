THORNSWOOD
==========

Thornswood is a co-op Skyrim roleplay server, in alpha and for people
eighteen and over. Anyone can install it, and the world is open by
application: you apply in the Discord, accept the rules, and the Playtester
role lets you press Play:
https://thornswood.net/discord

The full guide is https://thornswood.net/join. It is kept up to date, so if
it and this file ever disagree, the site is right.


What you need
-------------
- Skyrim Special Edition on Steam, on this PC. Anniversary Edition is not
  required.
- Vortex, with a free Nexus Mods account. The mods come from a collection on
  Nexus, and only Vortex can install a collection.
- Steam signed in and running.

Thornswood runs from a game folder of its own, C:\Thornswood\Game, which the
launcher builds from your Steam copy and what Vortex put into it. Most of it is
hard links to your own files, which take no extra space, and the few files
other programs rewrite are real copies. Tick "Give Thornswood a Skyrim folder
of its own" in the setup window and nothing is ever written into your Steam
copy: you can keep playing it, on any build, with your own mods. Leave it off
and Thornswood's own files go into your Steam copy until you turn the folder on
in the launcher, which then takes them back out.


Installing
----------
1. Run Thornswood-Setup.exe. Do not unzip anything yourself, it does that.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", press "More info", then
   "Run anyway". It fetches Thornswood's own content while you watch and puts
   the launcher in. Thornswood plays out of its own game folder, not out of
   your Steam copy, and the launcher builds that folder the first time you
   open it.

2. Open Thornswood from the desktop shortcut. The server runs Skyrim build
   1.6.1170 and most copies are newer. If yours is, a card on the launcher's
   front page says so. Press Downgrade and Steam downloads that build under
   your own account, for Thornswood's own game folder. Your Steam copy is
   not changed, so you can keep it on any build and mod it as you like. It
   is a few gigabytes, so give it a while.

3. Install the collection in Vortex. The link is on
   https://thornswood.net/downloads and on the launcher's Diagnostics page. This
   is not optional: without it you have a launcher and no mods, and the
   server turns you away when you press Play.

4. Press Connect Discord on the launcher's front page. Your browser opens,
   you say yes, and it closes itself. You only do this once.


Playing
-------
Always start from the Thornswood shortcut. Not Skyrim, not Steam, not SKSE.
The launcher takes any waiting update before it lets you play, because
everybody has to run the same files or nobody can connect. There is no
address to type and no password to remember.

Voice runs in the launcher, not the game, so leave the launcher open while
you play.

Do not run Steam's "Verify Integrity of Game Files" on Skyrim. It moves your
copy to the newest build, and the server runs 1.6.1170.


If something goes wrong
-----------------------
Look at https://thornswood.net/known and https://thornswood.net/help first.
If it is not there, say so in the Discord with what you were doing, what you
expected and what happened instead, or use https://thornswood.net/report.

The launcher's Logs page lists its own log and any crash logs, and opens
them. Drag either straight into the Discord channel.
