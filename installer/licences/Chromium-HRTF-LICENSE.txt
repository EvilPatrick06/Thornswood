The launcher's HRTF panner (launcher\app\VoiceHrtf.cs) is a C# port of
Chromium's Web Audio HRTF panning, as it was at Chromium commit
dd2ed7a22c23f9c9a85e9514c8c3308bf6ae4fbe (https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src):

  third_party/blink/renderer/platform/audio/hrtf_panner.cc and delay.cc,
  under the first notice below;

  third_party/blink/renderer/platform/audio/hrtf_elevation.cc,
  hrtf_kernel.cc, hrtf_database.cc, fft_frame.cc and fft_convolver.cc, under
  the second notice below;

  media/base/sinc_resampler.cc, third_party/blink/renderer/platform/audio/
  rustfft_ffi.rs and the azimuth and elevation of
  third_party/blink/renderer/modules/webaudio/panner_handler.cc, under
  Chromium's LICENSE, the third text below.

The impulse responses it filters with have their own notice,
IRCAM-Listen-HRTF.txt.
